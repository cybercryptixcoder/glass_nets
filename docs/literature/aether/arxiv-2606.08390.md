# When Are Neural Interaction Discoveries Real? Identifiability, Recoverability, and a Pre-Fit Diagnostic

- **Key:** arxiv-2606.08390
- **Authors / organisation:** Valentina V. Kuskova, Dmitry Zaytsev, Michael Coppedge; Lucy Family Institute for Data & Society and Department of Political Science, University of Notre Dame
- **Date:** 2026-06-07
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08390
- **Kind:** paper
- **Read from:** full text
- **Relevance to us:** 3 (central)

## What they wanted, and why

A neural time-series model that reports "variable *k* modulates the effect of variable *j* on the target" is making a mechanistic-sounding claim, and the claim is only worth anything if the recovered modulator is a property of the data rather than of one particular optimisation run. The authors argue the right frame for that worry is identifiability, not capacity: a rich enough model class can always fit the data while admitting several interaction decompositions that agree on the observed inputs and disagree everywhere else. The question they set out to answer is therefore not whether a model can *represent* effect modification but whether the interaction structure is *uniquely recoverable* from the observational distribution. Their headline structural claim is that the answer is governed by the geometry of the observed input support rather than by the architecture. The larger aim is a practitioner-facing test that can be run before fitting anything.

## What they studied

A single model class, G-NAVAR: a neural additive vector-autoregression in which each source's base contribution is multiplied by strictly positive gates driven by candidate modulators, so the conditional mean of the target is an intercept plus a sum of "edge products". The analysed instances are a five-variable stationary synthetic system with lag order two (about two thousand gate parameters, a saturation-times-change gate and a two-dimensional inhibition gate) and three real panels: hourly Beijing air quality at four monitoring sites, a World Development Indicators country panel spanning 1970–2023, and daily realized volatility for eight international equity indices over 2,615 trading days. The unit of analysis is the multiplicative factorisation — the edge product and its individual gates — not neurons or features of a transformer.

## How they did it

They first enumerate the transformations that leave the fitted function unchanged (rescaling the base function against its gates, permuting modulators, inserting a gate that is identically one) and remove them by normalising every gate to unit mean and requiring every listed modulator to be essential. They then prove two obstructions to recovery: under dependent inputs the conditional mean absorbs part of one source's effect as a function of another (so conditioning does not isolate sources), and under support that lives on a lower-dimensional set distinct gates can agree on the observed region while differing off it. With those established, they build sufficient conditions — edge contributions must live in pre-specified spaces that form a direct sum, the joint support of each modulator set must be a full-dimensional connected product set with positive density, and the gates must be continuous and strictly positive — and prove that observational equivalence then forces the base function and every gate to coincide, up to the removed gauges. A stronger version allows shared modulators by handing each edge only the hierarchically orthogonal functional-ANOVA components that contain its own source. To turn the support condition into something checkable, they take the effective rank (participation ratio of the spectrum) of the joint lag-block covariance, which is closed-form from one eigendecomposition, and pair it with a two-seed stability check on the recovered modulator ranking. Empirically they sweep a correlation between the two true modulators at fixed sample size, then apply the same pipeline to the three real panels, using held-out MSE against a capacity-matched additive baseline, a black-box MLP, and an additive-plus-pairwise functional-ANOVA model as controls.

## What counts as an explanation here

A conditional identifiability result for a named model class plus a decomposition structure: the explanation is "these gates, under these support and separability conditions, and only up to these gauges". It is deliberately partial — the structural modulation set is recoverable without extra assumptions, but the listed gate set requires a separate gate–interaction faithfulness assumption, which the authors state as a hypothesis rather than derive under dependence. The deliverable that is meant to be predictive in a stronger sense is the pre-fit effective-rank screen: a number computed from the inputs alone, before any training, that is asserted to predict whether recovery is feasible.

## How they judged success

Three measures, applied at three different levels. Theoretically: a population identifiability theorem with an explicit proof per claim, and a matching impossibility corollary for collapsed support. Computationally: the fraction of seeds recovering the true modulator set at four sample sizes and on a correlation sweep, with gauge-normalised gate error plotted directly against pre-fit effective rank rather than against the correlation knob. Empirically: held-out MSE against the capacity-matched baselines (with parameter counts stated so the comparison cannot be won by capacity starvation), top-ranked modulator margins, and inter-seed agreement on the top modulator, organised into a three-row taxonomy of regimes.

## What they found

The synthetic recovery curve is monotone in sample size: no seed recovers both modulators at one thousand samples, four of five at five thousand, and five of five by twenty-five thousand; gate error falls with data but plateaus well above zero, which the authors attribute to finite-support smoothing. Against an additive model with the same design the gated model wins by a factor of roughly seventeen in held-out MSE (0.0102 against 0.1716) at a hundred thousand samples and wins every seed, and the gated model is statistically indistinguishable from a black-box MLP and from an additive-plus-pairwise model at large sample sizes, so the interpretable parameterisation costs no accuracy — but the pairwise model recovers the true interacting pairs in 15 of 15 runs against the gated model's 12 of 15, with all three misses traced to one initialisation seed. The correlation sweep shows effective rank of the modulator block falling from 3.00 toward 1.54 as the modulators become collinear, with recovery collapsing to zero of ten seeds at the top of the range. On Beijing air quality, temperature is the top-ranked modulator of the NO₂→O₃ edge at all four sites with margins of 2.0 to 59.3 times the runner-up and effective rank above 4 throughout. On the development panel the effective rank is adequate (4.47 of a possible 4) but the two seeds disagree on the top modulator, the gate slopes change sign across seeds, and the gated model does not beat the additive baseline (1.26 against 1.19), so the pre-committed prediction is reported as not borne out. On realized volatility the effective rank is 1.49 to 1.97, the diagnostic flags collapse, seeds agree on the top modulator only 44% of the time, and the gated model wins in one of four cases — yet the within-fit margins are large (1.9 to 5.8 times), which the authors call its sharpest signature of non-identifiability: confident but arbitrary.

## Limits and open problems they name

The theorem is conditional on the hierarchically orthogonal decomposition's own regularity assumptions, which the connected-product-support condition does not imply; the shared-modulator version cannot handle a variable that is both a source for one edge and a modulator of another; and upgrading structural recovery to listed-gate recovery needs a faithfulness assumption that holds cleanly only under independence. The effective-rank screen is necessary but not sufficient: it cannot see non-linear degeneracy, cannot detect a missing modulator (it only scores the candidate set you handed it), and cannot detect order misspecification, where the true interaction is genuinely higher-order than the base-times-gates form. They name the order question explicitly as the deeper open problem: a three-way product restricted to a collinear support is exactly a two-way function, so interaction order itself becomes unidentifiable, and the paper states that a model with the wrong order is misspecified in a way no amount of data repairs.

## What we take from it

Directly reusable **framing**: identifiability of an interaction decomposition is a property of the input support, decidable before fitting, and it fails in a specific, checkable way — a necessary-but-not-sufficient pre-fit spectral screen paired with an asymmetrical post-fit two-seed check that licenses a negative conclusion firmly and a positive one only provisionally. Reusable **warning**: a large within-fit margin on an unidentifiable decomposition is *worse* evidence than a small margin, because it advertises confidence where the model is merely arbitrary — the volatility table is the folder's clean counterexample to reading "dominant modulator" off a single fit. Reusable **measure**: participation-ratio effective rank as a smooth count of how many directions the support actually occupies, chosen explicitly over condition number (which ignores the bulk of the spectrum) and over thresholded numerical rank (which is discontinuous). Reusable **negative result**: a synthesis that is exactly a lower-order function on the data's support, which is the sharpest statement in the folder that a decomposition's *order* can be an artifact of where the data lives. Read against run 745 (a signed component score whose zero point is set by a gauge) and run 743 (two instruments for the "same" causal quantity disagreeing, with the disagreement as the finding). Cross-reference run 740 (a decomposition that preserved only 26% of the target, gated behind a check) and run 744 (a decomposition whose cost is exponential in order).

Concretely: whenever our white-box model reports a component or interaction as a fact about the network, we should state the support on which it was measured, report a pre-fit rank or spread statistic for that support, and run the decomposition at least twice from independent initialisations with the between-run disagreement printed. A component recovered stably across two seeds is supporting evidence; a component recovered unstably, or stably under collapsed support, is not.

# Every Ablation Is a Dose: Counterweights and the Semblance of Self-Repair

- **Kind:** paper

- **Relevance to us:** 3 (central)

## What they wanted, and why

Ablation is how the field infers that a component matters, but ablating a component often makes other components appear to take over, and that apparent "self-repair" makes ablated importance an underestimate and misattributes what the ablated unit was doing. Earlier work catalogued instances (backup name movers, a "Hydra" effect, anti-erasure neurons) and the most systematic study concluded the process is noisy and has no single mechanism. These authors want the opposite conclusion: they argue the capacity is standing rather than recruited, and that a single law governs how a downstream unit's contribution changes as an upstream unit is disturbed. The larger goal is to make ablation an interpretable instrument by first making it a calibrated one: a deletion is one unmarked point on an axis, and without the axis you cannot tell a unit that switches on from a unit that opposes in proportion to what was removed.

## What they studied

Four instruction-tuned models from different families - Llama-3-8B-Instruct, Gemma-2-9b-it, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 - plus an out-of-domain replication on GPT-2 small. The task is a zero-shot True/False verdict on declarative statements built from 1,209 Known-Facts records, with a synthetic false partner made by swapping one type-matched attribute so the pair differs only in its asserted attribute; pairs are then filtered per model to those the model answers correctly on both sides (n between 478 and 598). The unit of analysis is a weight-space direction: an MLP neuron, an OV output channel, or a left-singular mode of an MLP's down-projection or of a head's OV circuit - each written as a fixed write vector plus a scalar activation coefficient, which lets the authors pin or clamp one direction at a time.

## How they did it

They first name a bench from the weights alone: each direction is labelled by the top and bottom tokens its write vector promotes and suppresses at the unembedding, and kept if a truth or falsity term (in any of several languages) appears at a pole - 6,310 such directions in Qwen, with the scan itself using no activations. Then two nested interventions. A primary clamps the "core" (the one or two directions that carry the verdict) to a continuous dose parameterised by the pair's own class contrast, with +1 the clean model, 0 the class-neutral midpoint, and -1 the full activation swap; a secondary, run inside that dosed world, patches a downstream direction's activation with the value it takes on the partner prompt *in the same dosed world*. The estimand is the margin erased by that secondary override, so the primary's shift cancels and only the downstream direction's contribution is read. They fit the erased margin against dose on three rungs (+1, 0, -1), hold out the half rungs and the overshoot rungs, and certify slopes with Benjamini-Hochberg control within each family. Finally they build a static, weights-only "wiring alignment" - the cosine between the core's write vector and a candidate's read rows, z-scored against 2,000 random readers from the same layer - and correlate it against the fitted slope. Every conventional clamp (zero, mean, resample, counterfactual patch) is shown to be a point on the same axis, with the dose chosen by the data rather than by the experimenter.

## What counts as an explanation here

A quantitative law with a static predictor: a component's contribution is an affine function of the dose applied to the upstream core, with one shared slope per direction (negative = counterweight, positive = relay) and one intercept per class; and the rank of the slope magnitude is anticipated by a checkpoint-only alignment statistic. The explanation is partial and explicitly ordinal: the wiring statistic ranks couplings but does not predict their numeric value, carries no sign, covers only the direct value pathway, and is confounded with the clean effect. It predicts behaviour under intervention for units the core can causally reach, and says nothing about units before it in the forward pass - which the authors treat as a prediction rather than a gap, and check.

## How they judged success

Four checks, all signed and paired. (1) **Affine fit and holdout**: weighted least squares on three rungs, scored on half rungs run after the fits were frozen, with out-of-sample R² and slope shift in standard-error units; a quadratic term is added as a competing explanation. (2) **Certification**: slope over its standard error surviving per-family Benjamini-Hochberg at q=0.05, applied to the *slope* rather than to R²; the paper notes that R² alone would certify directions whose slope is unresolved. (3) **A named competing null**: final-normalisation renormalisation, whose strict form predicts that no direction can change sign, that amplification predicts the *wrong* sign of recruitment on opposed directions, and that the gain share is bounded by |1-g|·|E⁰|; this is measured per family and class at every dose. (4) **A placement control built from published structure**: on IOI, the ten heads the core swap can reach versus the ten it cannot, divided by Wang et al.'s own circuit, plus a per-model permutation test on the wiring correlation and a Fisher combination across the four independently trained models.

## What they found

One or two directions carry just over half the clean total effect in every family (52.7% Llama, 53.9% Gemma, 54.9% Qwen, 56.6% Mistral). Most of the reachable bench couples to the core: 68 of 81 downstream directions have a slope reliably different from zero, 52 counterweights against 16 relays. Fitted on three doses and tested on the half rungs it never saw, the line reaches out-of-sample R² of 0.65 to 0.91 by family, with the slope moving by 0.26 to 0.51 of its own standard error when the new rungs are folded in and the sign preserved on all 81 directions; a freed quadratic term moves the slope by less than its standard error, though the authors state that this does not establish the affine form over other smooth forms. The wiring statistic correlates with the slope magnitude at Spearman rho between 0.55 and 0.82 by model, Fisher-combined to about 1e-10, and survives refitting the slope on intervened rungs only. Renormalisation is excluded: the gain is elevated in no family-class cell above 1.03 at the on-manifold dose, is dose-*flat* where largest (Gemma's fake side, 1.080 to 1.103), moves the wrong way on true statements in both joint-core families, has the wrong predicted sign in half of the audited cells, and accounts for at most 4.5% of observed recruitment. The replication on GPT-2 small's IOI circuit, with a component set fixed by other authors, finds seven of ten reachable heads certifying, every one a counterweight, the largest on the copy-suppression head L10.h7 (slope -0.886, roughly twenty times the reachable median), a clean gap between reachable and unreachable heads (8-fold on the median once the caveat about a single dose is honoured), and the one curved exception becomes affine - R² 0.413 to 0.972 - once its mediating head is clamped into the core.

## Limits and open problems they name

One task at one answer position, read as a two-token verdict; generative tasks are untouched and explicitly open. The bench is restricted to legible monosemantic directions, though nothing requires the relevant units to be legible. In two-core models the downstream response is larger than the sum of the single-core responses, which the authors read as evidence that a counterweight can respond to distributed coupling - mapping those interactions is left for future work. The coupling law is certified only inside the on-manifold window and saturates outside it; the exact coefficient is not recoverable from the weights, only its rank. Appendix L adds that the placement control leans on one dose, and that the honest reachable-versus-unreachable separation is about eightfold rather than the orders of magnitude the raw medians suggest. Whether recruitment runs through re-expression or re-routing is left open, and is framed as internal to an effect whose existence is already fixed causally. Their own closing reflection turns the critique on themselves: a tool can be entangled with structure it does not know about, and their instrument deserves the same scrutiny they applied to ablation.

## What we take from it

A method, a measure, and a warning. **Method:** replace the ablation-versus-nothing comparison with a *dose ladder* whose scale is the model's own contrast for the item, and read every component's contribution inside the dosed world rather than comparing across worlds - because a single deletion point cannot distinguish a gate that switches on from a proportional opposition. **Measure:** report, for every component, an intercept (its standing contribution) and a slope (how its contribution moves with the upstream dose), plus the *crossing dose* at which its sign flips, since that is the quantity that decides whether an intervention will look like repair; and use a holdout *inside* the window rather than only extrapolation, which is what separates a line from a family of smooth curves. **Predictive shortcut:** a checkpoint-only alignment between an upstream write and a downstream read ranks the coupling magnitudes without a single forward pass (rho 0.55-0.82 across independently trained models) - this is the shape of predictor a white-box model should be judged against, and it demonstrates the right evaluation: rank correlation across units within a model, combined across models, with a stated causal-reach boundary. **Warning:** the paper's own vocabulary is the useful part for us - it insists that an intervention is a *measurement instrument* with a calibration curve, and that an uncalibrated instrument manufactures phenomena. Two concrete guards follow: (a) a pass-through null (here, the final normalisation gain, whose predicted sign profile is wrong in half the cells) must be tested against the *dose profile* and not only against the sign, because a constant multiplier counterfeits a small recruitment but cannot counterfeit a graded one; (b) a placement control - units the intervention structurally cannot reach - must be run and reported with its own limited separation, since the unreachable set is where a wiring statistic would otherwise be free to be right for the wrong reason. Cross-reference run 751 (a screen that returns a correct-looking pattern with the opposite causal sign) and run 750 (a proposal stage passing its null while the verdict inverted).

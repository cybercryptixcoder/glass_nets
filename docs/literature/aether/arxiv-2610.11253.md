## What they wanted, and why
An agent that searches for a mechanistic model eventually stops improving, and the stop is ambiguous: either the proposer is too weak, or the data it is allowed to collect cannot in principle determine the parameters it is asking for. The paper's claim is that the second case is a computable fact rather than a mystery, and that an agent should compute it instead of guessing. The motivation is the obvious failure of the alternative — an agent that cannot tell the two apart either keeps refining against a target its data cannot constrain, or reports a confident model with no predictive power. The larger goal is to put a classical identifiability and experimental-design apparatus (sensitivity rank, Fisher conditioning, symmetries of a design class) inside an LLM agent's loop, as its stopping rule and its experiment-selection rule, and to state exactly when the stop is a proof. A secondary but sharp goal is to turn the same instrument outward, onto the benchmark's own scoring metric, and ask which distinctions that metric rewards are visible to any experiment the agent may run.

## What they studied
Unit of analysis: **parameters of a proposed model** and the **columns of a sensitivity (Jacobian) matrix** — that is, parameter directions in a hypothesis, not network weights, neurons or features. There is no trained neural network anywhere in the measurement path; the proposer is a five-role LLM "Agentic Council" (theorist, empiricist, contrarian, synthesizer, replicator, plus a deterministic fitting engine), and every score is computed in numpy/sympy/scipy behind a check that forbids a model client in the scoring path. Testbeds: a purpose-built two-body "Alien Universe" whose force law has a hidden per-body property; the 63 ODEBench systems in the pinned LLM-ACES release; textbook systems (four-parameter Lotka–Volterra, Van der Pol, Lorenz, an oral one-compartment pharmacokinetic model, Michaelis–Menten); integer-sequence identities; and an audit of the eleven public worlds of the DiscoverPhysics benchmark.

## How they did it
1. **Diagnose.** Represent the model as a map from parameters to observations and compute its sensitivity matrix by central finite differences, or by forward sensitivity equations for ODEs. Full rank with a condition number at or below 10^8 is called identifiable; a rank drop names the null-space direction as the confounded combination; full rank but a condition number above that bound is called practically non-identifiable. Ranks on the ODE suite are read in log-parameter coordinates because a fixed relative floor on singular values is not scale-invariant. For rational/ODE forms an exact symbolic test runs alongside.
2. **Certify the class, not the design.** The novelty is a certificate that asks whether the confounded direction is a symmetry of *every* design the agent is allowed to run. The symbolic route keeps the design controls (times, radii, doses, initial conditions) symbolic, snaps the numeric null vector to small rationals, and tests whether the directional derivative of the prediction along it reduces to literal zero; if so, no design in the class can see it. The numeric route stacks sensitivity matrices over many sampled designs and requires the normalized response to fall at or below 1e-7, which is evidence over the sampled designs, not a proof.
3. **Enforce regularity.** A first-order test alone is unsound. The paper's own counterexample is f = b³x² with x > 0 at b = 0, where every design has zero derivative in b yet b is injective-identifiable; the class rank is 0 at the point and 1 nearby. Both certificate routes therefore now refuse when the class rank rises at nearby perturbed parameter points.
4. **Triage the plateau.** Identifiable current design means a capability limit; non-identifiable but uncertified means resolvable inside the class, so the ranker picks the next design; every confounded direction certified means change the class and the loop terminates at round zero, spending no proposer calls.
5. **Rank candidate designs** by how many parameter directions they restore, with conditioning as a sub-unit tie-break.
6. **Build a testbed with a switch.** In the Alien Universe the two-body force has an inverse-square plus a short-range correction, and the repulsive term depends on charges only through their product, so equal-product configurations integrate to bit-identical trajectories. Observing one configuration makes the product a single lumped constant (non-identifiable); observing a family with distinct products makes the structure testable. The identifiable arm's prompt also states that a per-body property exists and names its symbols — a change of task text, not only of data.
7. **Cross-check and audit.** Point the unchanged deterministic engine at systems the authors did not build; sweep the competitor's benchmark data; compare selection criteria head-to-head on identical candidate designs with measured outcomes; and run the certificate over every top-band claim in a published benchmark's own explanation rubric.

## What counts as an explanation here
A **verdict plus a named direction**: "these parameters are confounded, this is the confounded combination as a vector, and this is whether any experiment of the current kind can separate them." The object is not a description of a computation but a statement of what a model-plus-design *cannot* determine — a completeness claim of the negative kind, scoped to a declared design class and a declared operating point. It is meant to be partial by construction and the paper says so: the certificate is only as good as the declared class (drawn too narrowly, it reports exhaustion where a wider class would have resolved the parameter) and only as good as the operating point (local, so it cannot see a second discrete solution). It does predict: a certified direction must be invisible to every design in the class, which the authors test by showing that rollouts from two parameter values displaced along the certified direction agree to 5e-14 from every candidate initial condition. A derived lemma also says any score computed only from in-class predictions is equally blind to a certified direction, and that is the lemma the DiscoverPhysics audit tests against a leaderboard.

## How they judged success
Correctness of the diagnostic is checked on systems with settled answers: textbook ODEs under full-state and single-coordinate designs with rank, verdict and condition number tabulated; a planted Lotka–Volterra variant where two parameters enter only through a product and two others only through a sum, where the recovered null directions come back as the expected ±0.707 patterns; a constructed eight-model battery with known answers, run to characterize the symbolic checker; and a published pharmacokinetic non-identifiability, where the ranker is asked to pick among three follow-up designs. Agent-loop measurements use **eight seeds per cell, paired arms, mean paired difference with a 95% bootstrap interval, one-sided Wilcoxon in the pre-registered direction, Cliff's delta, and Holm–Bonferroni within a prediction family**, with predictions and bands committed before the runs and failed predictions reported as failed. The criterion comparison is outcome-measured rather than modelled: round-zero data plus each candidate design at 1% noise, Levenberg–Marquardt fits from perturbed starts, root-mean-square log-parameter error over five noise seeds, against A-optimal, D-optimal, expected-information-gain, disagreement and random. The benchmark audit uses Cramér–Rao standard errors for parameter claims, joint fits of a named rival over 65 legal experiments for mechanism claims, and a Fisher exact test across cells of the published leaderboard.

## What they found
**The engine reproduces settled answers and finds one dead end in a competitor's benchmark.** On the 62 ODEBench systems with free constants, 60 are identifiable at round zero (exact-binomial 95% CI 0.89–1.00, median condition number 12), none is practically non-identifiable, and two are rank-deficient in raw coordinates. The RC circuit's three constants enter only through two combinations, so no initial condition and no time window can separate them; the certificate proves the vector field invariant along the null direction for every state, and two models displaced along it give rollouts agreeing to 5e-14 from every candidate initial condition — a disagreement-based acquisition has nothing to act on there. A harvesting model is merely badly conditioned and is improved about a hundredfold by one box initial condition. **The textbook table is not uniformly confirmatory**: with the initial condition known, prey-only Lotka–Volterra is locally identifiable at rank 4 of 4 (condition numbers 124, 169 and 361 over horizons [0,4], [0,20], [0,40]), while a differential-algebra tool reports a scaling non-identifiability once the unobserved predator initial condition is unknown; the diagnostic holds the initial condition fixed and does not see it, and the paper prints both. On the pharmacokinetic model the diagnostic returns rank 3 of 4 with the (V, F) scaling axis at cosine 1.000000, four-times denser oral sampling and a 72-hour window both stay at rank 3, and only the intravenous arm reaches rank 4 (condition number 1.2e2) — the design pharmacologists use. The flip-flop reparameterisation is a second solution the local check cannot see; the intravenous arm separates it at a maximum difference of 1.7 against 0.0. **The switch works as designed and the paper demotes its own headline**: depth at least three fires on 8/8 treatment seeds (mean 3.25) against 1/8 control (mean 1.875), one-sided p = 0.0078 with one tied pair, Cliff's delta 0.906, mean paired difference 1.375 [0.75, 2.00]; under a symmetric post-hoc control gate the single credited control seed drops and the numbers become 0/8, delta 1.0, p = 0.0039. The factorial separates the factors: family-only passes the cross-configuration layer 16/16 against 4/16 without (Fisher p = 1.6e-5), the fitting engine passes the correction-exponent layer 16/16 against 8/16 (p = 0.0024), and consecutive depth three is reached by 2/8 family-only and 1/8 engine-only against 8/8 with both. **Second-regime transfer is partly a negative result**: with the probe battery corrected the cross-configuration gate holds at 7/8 but the exponent-banded layer falls from 6/8 to 4/8 and then to 2/8, and depth at least three falls to 3/8, below the registered bar — a more data-faithful engine scores worse on a truth-centred band because the data-optimal exponent is biased low by the hidden term. **The architecture comparison dissolves under a fairer gate**: the five-agent Council beats a single agent at depth 3 versus depth 1 on all eight equal-wall-clock seeds, but the separation is carried entirely by the correction-exponent layer, and rescoring the same stored hypotheses under a gate that fits each proposal's own exponent to held-out acceleration gives Cliff's delta 0. The call-matched control is honest about its own floor: at 15 calls both arms tie at depth 1, at 40 calls the Council reaches depth 3 or better on 5/8 and the single agent never leaves depth 1 (raw p ≈ 0.031, delta 0.625) but the registered Holm plan is unsatisfiable because the all-tie cell caps the adjusted p at 0.0625, and the authors say they do not claim it. What survives is a mechanism rather than an architecture: terminal accuracy tracks how often the deterministic engine's feedback was delivered, from 0/8 at near-zero deliveries to 4/8 at one and 7/8 at many. **The benchmark audit finds two worlds grading an impossible distinction**: the fractional-Laplacian force is exactly a power law under a reparameterisation that sympy verifies in both directions and the simulated forces agree to 2e-16, so the rubric's top band rewards naming the operator rather than the power law, which no experiment in the complex interface can resolve; every model with accurate trajectories failed the explanation grade in those worlds, 15 of 15 cells, against 5 of 9 in identifiable worlds (one-sided Fisher p = 0.012). Seven of nine registered world verdicts matched and two were falsified (gravity and yukawa are resolvable only by non-default experiments because a named rival matches the truth), and the predicted ordering of per-world ceilings was not supported (Mann–Whitney p = 0.34). **The ranker is not the best selection rule**: A-optimal design had the lowest median error and was the only criterion significantly better than rank-then-conditioning (64 tasks better, 14 worse, Holm-adjusted p = 4.9e-7), D-optimal, the hybrid and information gain were statistically indistinguishable from it, disagreement was the weakest model-based criterion and random was worst, and on the certified RC circuit every criterion but the certificate spends an experiment per round on a task no experiment can resolve.

## Limits and open problems they name
The switch confounds protocol with prompt — the family arm's task text announces the hidden property and names its symbols, so the lift is a joint effect and a clean replication would state the latent property in both arms or in neither. Seeds share a distilled memory of judging criteria that is not run-scoped, so the paired tests assume an independence the runs do not have and every p-value on the live-agent cells is optimistic by an unmeasured amount. The factorial straddles two provider rosters because a model reached end of life mid-batch. The diagnostic is local and cannot see a second discrete solution, and its answer depends on what is declared known. Numerical rank depends on parameter scaling. The live loop uses a two-move vocabulary, five scored calls and a constructed plateau, which is a capability demonstration and not a powered measurement. The integer-sequence identities are elementary textbook facts, the search covered OEIS windows only and missed the probability literature, and the certifier is sound at literal zero and incomplete elsewhere — one true identity came back unverified on a symbolic non-decision. The numeric certificate route has a floor at the integrator's finite-difference noise: on a second platform the stacked response came back at 1.2e-7 against a registered 1e-7 bar, so that one pre-registered prediction is counted as platform-dependent. And a benchmark audit can only report the claims it could parse: one world is unfinished because a registered joint fit did not complete, and time-evolving rivals that the benchmark's own engine cannot simulate were not scored.

## What we take from it
1. **A discipline, not just a metric: certify what no experiment of the class can show, and refuse to certify at a singular point.** This is the first entry in this folder to give a formal soundness condition for a stopping rule, and the counterexample is the part to copy — a first-order null direction at a rank-deficient parameter value can be perfectly identifiable, so any test our account uses to say "this unit is invisible to everything we can do" must be paired with a regularity check that the rank is constant in a neighbourhood, and must refuse rather than conclude when it is not. Concretely: before our account declares a unit or a direction unavailable, compute the diagnostic at the operating point *and* at nearby perturbed points, and report the verdict as conditional on regularity.
2. **The blindness lemma is the cleanest warning the folder has collected about scoring our own account.** Any score computed only from predictions the agent itself can elicit is provably constant along a certified direction, so it can neither reward nor penalize getting that direction right. The mechanical rule for us: **when our account's grade is a held-out error computed on inputs drawn from the same design class the account was fitted with, that grade is blind to exactly the directions the account cannot see, and the write-up says so where the number is printed; a test of whether our account explains a failure must use an out-of-class or parameter-level metric.** This is `arxiv-2611.17879`'s held-out-transition collapse stated as a theorem rather than observed as an empirical gap.
3. **A certified dead end is a budget statement, and the paper prices it against selection rules.** Every selection criterion spends an experiment per round on a task no experiment can resolve; the certificate spends none. In our terms: an account of "which unit to probe next" should be preceded by a cheap deterministic check of "can any probe of this kind resolve this unit at all", and when the answer is no the correct output is the identifiable combination, not another probe. A-optimal design being the better tie-break is a reminder that feasibility and precision are separate questions, and the paper recommends a combination it explicitly did not pre-register or test as a unit.
4. **A negative lesson about the metrology of our own evaluation.** Three of the four predictions about the paper's own depth metric failed, and the failure was informative: the collapse was not a local conditioning effect but a displaced indifference band, so the gate passed a wrong exponent because its threshold was permissive rather than because the data preferred the truth. Mechanical rule: **write the quantity an evaluation should resolve as a free parameter, run the identifiability diagnostic on the evaluation's own observation design, and compute its indifference band before paying for a measurement** — a metric whose indifference band does not contain the alternative it is supposed to exclude is an unmade distinction.

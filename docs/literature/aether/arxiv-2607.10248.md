# One mechanism for many mental spaces: a shared router over a value slot in language models

- **Key:** arxiv-2607.10248
- **Authors / organisation:** Oliver Steele, Jiangtao Wen, Yuxing Han; Tsinghua University + NYU Shanghai
- **Date:** 2026-07-11 (v1); v2 revised 2026-07-21
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10248
- **Kind:** paper
- **Read from:** full text (PDF text layer, v2, 26 pages)
- **Relevance to us:** 3 (central)

## What they wanted, and why
Language routinely describes one entity with different values in different non-actual contexts: a flower red in reality and purple in a portrait, a cup blue in reality and green in a painting, a jar that is beer in one character's belief and coffee in another's. Formal semantics keeps those contexts in separate categories because their inference rules differ, while Fauconnier's mental-space theory treats them as one operation of building a space and binding values inside it. The authors ask which of the two a transformer language model actually implements, and whether the context itself is represented as an object with its own vehicle, or only implicitly through the values it governs. The larger goal is to locate a discourse-context variable inside the network and show it is causally used, not merely decodable. A companion paper is said to develop the belief case in depth; this one is the cross-type generality study.

## What they studied
Open-weight transformer language models: Qwen2.5-3B (36 layers) as the primary substrate, with replication on Pythia-2.8b (32 layers) and Falcon3-3B (22 layers), three unrelated families, plus Qwen2.5-14B, Qwen2.5-7B/14B-Instruct, OLMo-2-7B-Instruct and Mistral-Small-24B for later parts. GPT-2 was used only to sanity-check the intervention machinery and is not reported because it applies the rule at chance. The unit of analysis is a low-rank subspace of the residual stream at the final entity token (and separately at the value token), factored into a *value slot* holding an attributed value and a *router* (space index) that selects which space a query reads. Stimuli: one entity, one attribute (colour, four values, chance 0.25), a base-world clause and an alternative clause in random order, followed by a neutral probe ending on the entity; nine builder types (painting, belief, dream, movie, past, yesterday, hypothetical, modal, reported speech) plus an actor-to-character connector, 240 items per condition.

## How they did it
Linear probes — standardised multinomial logistic regression on the residual stream at the entity token, 5-fold stratified cross-validation, out-of-fold accuracy with 95% confidence intervals — test whether the base and alternative values both decode at a later mention, with base-only and shuffled-label controls. Diff-of-means steering at the maintained-representation layer is compared against twenty matched-norm random directions, each given the same coefficient-selection procedure as the real vector, because single random draws were found to be unreliable. Distributed Alignment Search learns an orthonormal rank-k subspace with the model frozen (Adam at 5e-3, about 150 steps, batch 16) under an interchange objective that maximises the donor space's value at the readout; rank is swept, and every causal number is reported as a margin over a rank-1 diff-of-means splice and a random-subspace floor. Cross-type transfer is measured by training on one builder type and applying the subspace unchanged to every other, with a transfer index normalised between the matched random floor and the within-type ceiling, and a bar of 0.7 fixed in advance. Output writing is located with a logit lens and with head-level direct logit attribution. A rule-consequent task (colour to recipient) separates the value the model reports from the value it uses downstream. Confound controls include neutral padding, base-only and shuffled labels, two-entity context-copy, cross-entity space index, space-versus-order crossing, property-general anchors, entity-type anchors, four surface frames per builder, capacity and swap errors, and a frontier-model behavioural battery.

## What counts as an explanation here
A causal abstraction at the level of a small number of named variables: a shared low-rank subspace that tags which discourse space a value belongs to, factored into a router at the query token and a slot at the value token, with additive composition against entity identity. The object is deliberately partial — it explains which of several coexisting values a query emits and how a built space is indexed, not the semantics of the sentence. It makes predictions a mere fit would not: one trained subspace controlling other space types, a double dissociation between report and reasoning, and a multi-rank capacity relation (geometric rank five-to-one against causal rank of four or less). The authors explicitly separate their object from entity binding, from character-object-state belief tracking, and from truth-direction probes.

## How they judged success
Out-of-fold probe accuracy with 95% confidence interval lower bound above chance; flip rate on held-out items under DAS interchange; the transfer index with its pre-fixed 0.7 bar; the leak-free 0.583 random and hand-built floor for causal control; principal-angle cosines and additive R-squared for the factorisation; shuffled-label and base-only controls for probes; logit-lens elevation over the two palette colours never mentioned in the item; behavioural accuracy and swap-error counts at frontier scale. Statistics are three-seed ranges on the main tables and single-seed runs on the narrowest claims, which the authors say.

## What they found
Both values stay decodable at a later mention on Qwen: 0.96 for the base colour and 0.94 for the painting colour, against chance 0.25, with the alternative-space colour decoding at 0.89 to 0.95 across the nine builder types and surviving padding (often anti-recency). The tag is entity-general (cross-entity probe transfer 0.88 to 1.00), property-general (0.83 on Qwen and 0.89 on Pythia across non-colour attributes) and entity-type-general (1.00 and 0.99), and it survives four surface frames per builder with best-layer accuracy 0.69 to 0.84 against shuffled-label controls at 0.25 to 0.27. Per-type alt-versus-reality directions have effective rank about four to five with one dominant shared component of roughly 40% of variance and cross-type probe transfer near 0.60. Entity times space factorises additively, R-squared 0.92 to 0.95 with principal cosine 0.29 to 0.34. Output writing is localised to a few late heads per model (Qwen L31-33, Pythia L16-20, Falcon3 L19-21) with disjoint mover heads for the two spaces. Hand-built steering is indistinguishable from random: at L34 the steered flip 0.48 sits inside the twenty-draw random band of 0.16 to 0.58, and a rank-1 diff-of-means splice sits exactly at the 0.583 leakage floor; learned DAS gives 0.883 at rank 1, 0.967 at rank 2 and 1.000 at rank 4, with rank-1 sufficient on Pythia and Falcon3. Cross-type transfer reaches index 0.73 on Qwen, 0.885 on Pythia and 0.711 on Falcon3 (rank-8), a random rank-8 subspace flipping only 0.28 to 0.38, and rank 4 matching rank 8 within 0.06 on every family; a past-trained subspace also flips prospective tense at 0.81, 0.90 and 0.83. Report and reasoning dissociate on Qwen: a colour-trained subspace flips the report 1.00 and the rule consequent 0.00, a consequent-trained subspace flips the consequent 1.00 and the report only 0.32, with a mean principal cosine between the subspaces of 0.11 (0.17 on Falcon3, which shows a messier 1.00/0.49 versus 0.29/1.00). Inside each space, asserted and derived values share a low-rank binding core, and derived subspaces carry a cross-space route signature (belief-derived to depictive-derived 0.996) that asserted subspaces do not. Building a compound space reuses the base slot (0.90 minimum transfer over a 0.03 floor) while minting a distinct router (cross-transfer 0.00, cosine at most 0.26), replicated over four compound types and two architectures. Within the shared subspace a coarse class taxonomy survives (within-class transfer 0.89 to 0.92 against between-class 0.53 to 0.76), but belief is not specially separated from the counterfactual: its transfer sits at or above the between-class baseline on every model, in a range where a boundary of the detected size (+0.16 to +0.37) would have been visible. Behaviourally, frontier models maintain eight coexisting spaces with no swap errors and apply the derived rule under load, while a smaller model retains stated bindings yet fails derived ones.

## Limits and open problems they name
The binding is retrieval-shaped rather than a persistent slot: at a two-entity scene the target's colours decode only marginally better than the distractor's (+0.07 Qwen, +0.09 Pythia) and the advantage decays to about zero within sixteen filler sentences, and this leaky explicit-frame readout is why the base-versus-image entanglement is only a partial double dissociation. All mechanistic results are on models of 3B parameters or fewer, with frontier behaviour checked only through an API that exposes no activations. The report-versus-reasoning test rests on one colour-sorting rule, and Pythia cannot run it. The construction result is mostly on instruction-tuned models, and the slot-reuse magnitude is single-substrate. Referential opacity itself is never tested (no co-referential substitution or de-re/de-dicto contrast), and modal possibility is the one builder the model fails to separate from the actual ("X might believe" collapses onto the actual colour). Whether two marked spaces' routers dissociate pairwise is left open, as is whether the built router has recoverable constituent structure and whether space indexing and entity binding are instances of one higher-level causal model.

## What we take from it
- **Instrument — the leakage floor as a required row.** Their honest control is the disturbing one: a full-magnitude rank-1 splice of the donor anchor leaks the answer through *any* direction at 0.55 to 0.58, so the causal claim lives entirely in the margin above that. Adopt: every intervention number we report sits next to the same-shaped non-mechanistic splice, and a hand-built direction that lands inside the matched twenty-draw random band is reported as no evidence at all.
- **Instrument — factorise the object and test the two loci separately.** Slot at the value token, router at the query token; the slot transfers almost fully across frames while the routers are near-orthogonal. This is a cheap way to convert "one mechanism" into a testable two-part decomposition, and their additive entity-times-space R-squared is the reporting shape for any of our objects that claim to compose.
- **Warning — a null result can confirm a theory.** They argue the missing belief boundary is what Fauconnier's account predicts, while the same null would be a failure for formal semantics; they also show the method does resolve class structure of comparable size, so the null is informative. Useful pattern: state what magnitude the instrument can detect before interpreting its silence.
- **Warning — decodable throughout, active only locally.** The stored-versus-active split (probe decodes everywhere, but the intervention is local and the advantage decays with distance) is the shape we should expect from our own probes, and a probe that generalises across entities and properties can still be reading a re-read-from-source computation rather than a persistent state.
- **Reusable dataset and method.** The Mental Spaces Corpus plus build code is released, and the diagnostic-stimulus style — a base clause, an alternative clause, a neutral probe ending on the entity — is a ready template for testing whether our model keeps coexisting values apart.

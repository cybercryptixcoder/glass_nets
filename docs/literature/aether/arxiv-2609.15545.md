## What they wanted, and why

Induction heads are the field's most-worn example of a circuit, and almost everything written about them assumes a single lookup on a full-attention transformer. This paper asks the three questions that framing leaves open: how does *training* build the path that moves the previous token's information to the historical value position, given that other computations (a recurrent state readout) can already lower the loss; how does one address incorporate context further back when several records share the same immediate predecessor; and how does one retrieved value become the query for the next retrieval. The authors want a decomposition of "a circuit" into three separable learning problems — forming, enriching, composing — because a model can possess an induction stage and still never learn to use it.

## What they studied

Hybrid architectures, which interleave linear-recurrent (DeltaNet / Gated DeltaNet) or sliding-window attention blocks with global attention. Three tiers: tiny controlled memory–attention models (d=128, 4 heads, FFN 256), pretrained 79M (8 layers, width 512) and 318M (16 layers, width 1024) hybrids trained 30K steps on packed 1,024-token OpenWebText, and released Qwen3-4B / Qwen3.5-4B. The unit of analysis is a *retrieval stage*: a local preparation block plus the global-attention receiver that follows it, with the attention head and the residual-block update as the intervention sites. Small (150,592-parameter) hybrids carry the semantic-extension experiments.

## How they did it

Three probes over the residual block update `u_l(p) = h_l(p) - h_{l-1}(p)`: **Carrying** changes the predecessor (or a lag-τ token) and patches the historical value position with an RMS-matched donor update, measuring the margin drop; **Matching** swaps historical associations and patches the query update; **Copying** replaces only the value-readout contribution at the query while holding keys and attention fixed. A fourth intervention, source-key restoration, patches the clean key at the historical value position back into a corrupted run and scores recovery as restored gain over disrupted loss, with source-value, off-site-key and query patches as specificity controls. Formation is studied by (a) pretraining trajectory timing across window widths and architecture families, (b) sweeping a memory block's retention γ and readout gain g and decomposing the gradient on the shared short convolution into local-branch and state-branch terms, (c) two direct decoupling interventions (gradient orthogonalisation and tap isolation), and (d) an initialisation scheme, SoftPrior, that biases a quarter of heads toward current-token state processing and the rest toward predecessor carrying at matched filter norm. Enrichment is studied by adapting only the 16 cue-token embeddings of a frozen literal induction circuit to category matching, then adding conflicting same-category records and intervening on distractor keys versus values. Composition is studied with a seven-configuration training-route suite on a two-hop task, natural-corpus and Python-AST audits of how often a two-hop candidate survives shortcut filters, and StateBench-inspired state tracking plus four compositional in-context tasks across three execution routes (stacked stages, weight-shared loops, one generated intermediate token).

## What counts as an explanation here

A **functional interface** rather than a circuit diagram: the historical source key `K_B1 = A_r(X)_B1` is declared to be the causal interface between local preparation and global retrieval, and the explanation is that three different task requirements (address context radius r, sequential retrieval depth k) decompose the architecture's behaviour into three learnable pieces that can each be measured by a patch. The account is deliberately partial — the authors say the source-key test measures a functional receiver interface, and that checkpoint lesions establish necessity at those checkpoints while the controlled gradient experiments isolate developmental interference. The predictive content is structural, not per-input: the r/k framework is used to derive the local-horizon bound `R_local ≤ L(W-1)` and to predict which reading the model must be using from where the margin effects sit.

## How they judged success

Logit-margin changes under patching (clean minus patched), restoration recovery percentages, intervened-minus-clean margins for lesions (Table II), full-vocabulary argmax accuracy on controlled tasks (five seeds, 128 prompts each), candidate-pair accuracy alongside full-vocabulary accuracy, paired-prompt accuracy with 95% bootstrap intervals and paired-accuracy bounds for Qwen, training-seed means ± SD with three seeds for the pretraining comparisons, t-statistics against stated three-seed resolution thresholds, §-level specifics controls (source value, off-site key, query), corpus audits with cumulative filter funnels (800 OpenWebText candidates, 42 Python aliases), and permutation-free but explicit controls such as swapped-lag initialisation, isotropic norm-matched initialisation, off-candidate logit biases, and value-consistency control grids that distinguish reading record 1 from reading record 2.

## What they found

Source-key restoration recovers retrieval almost completely across every setting: 100.0% recovery in the controlled pair (loss 4.42 ± 0.81, gain +4.42 ± 0.80), 98.2% in the controlled 3:1 block, 98.9% in the 79M recurrent hybrid, 83.1% in the 318M hybrid (with a separate single-receiver test at 80.5% NLL recovery), 98.3% for Qwen3.5-4B, and 100.2% / 100.5% in the 79M sliding-window hybrids. The specificity controls are near-null by comparison: source-value patches move the margin by at most +0.17, off-site-key patches are negative, and query patches are exactly 0.00. Formation: the recurrent state readout is a genuine alternative route — increasing retention and readout gain moves recall from cross-layer carrying (7.07 → 0.00) to intra-layer query state readout (0.00 → 24.67) — and same-layer separation of the shared taps (orthogonalisation, tap isolation) raises carrying across seeds. Pretrained repertoire: lesioning the value-path history at the source window leaves recall largely intact while lesioning the query-path history destroys it (79M recall margin 2.54 clean → −2.69 with Q history removed, versus −0.34 with V history removed). SoftPrior raises earlier-receiver Matching in 6/6 seeds across both scales with no statistically resolvable perplexity change, and trades near-range for distant recall in 3/3 seeds at 79M. Enrichment: training only the 16 cue embeddings takes category accuracy from 36.1 ± 5.0% to 100.0%, and both the adapted query and the adapted keys are needed for transfer (50.6% and 62.0% alone, 100.0% together, 36.1% for values). Conflicting same-category records suppress recall while cross-category padding does not, and swapping distractor keys (but not values) removes or introduces the failure. A single-read model shows the sharp boundary the theory predicts: at a window of 2, in-horizon lag-3 carrying is still positive while out-of-horizon lag-4 carrying in the pre-receiver stack is identically zero. Composition: five of seven training routes reach at most 0.508 on unseen two-hop pairs (chance 0.500) — including two trained stages without two-hop supervision and a model trained from scratch on endpoints — while the aligned route reaches 0.980 and a swap of the first-hop intermediate moves the endpoint margin by 19.15. State tracking collapses the same way: endpoint-only two-stage, looped and latent-token arms sit near 0.25-0.51 on the group-accumulator and reference tiers, while the aligned versions of all three routes reach 0.98-1.00, and one-token CoT reaches 1.000 across every tier and length. The corpus audits explain why: the shortcut-free two-hop funnel runs 800 → 613 → 76 → 3 → 0, and every one of the 6 surviving OpenWebText candidates is a repeated span, while PyTorch AST audit finds 0 of 42 conflict-free two-hop instances.

## Limits and open problems they name

The controlled studies are small (d=128, 150k parameters) and the pretrained ones stop at 318M plus two released Qwen checkpoints; the causal arm at 318M sums two receiver-specific gains into a descriptive aggregate. The source-key restoration establishes necessity at evaluated checkpoints, not a developmental story. The single-read visibility result is a theorem under specified access restrictions. The corpus audits characterise only the sampled windows (800 OpenWebText candidates, 42 Python aliases), and continued relational training on the 318M hybrid improved recombination inside trained systems while remaining weak on unseen notations and raising code perplexity from 34.71 to 38.73. SoftPrior's effect on perplexity is below what three seeds can resolve — they state that detecting a 1-2 PPL effect needs 8-10 seeds per scale — so the training intervention is supported by circuit-level readouts, not by a language-modelling win.

## What we take from it

A candidate naming scheme and a **cheap classifier for the failure mode we keep hitting**, which is a reading that explains a mechanism the model possesses but does not use. Three things transfer directly. (1) *Two requirements as two numbers.* "Address radius" and "retrieval depth" turn a vague question about a circuit's complexity into two measurable coordinates, and the companion decomposition of a stage into carrying / matching / copying gives three separate patch tests whose controls (value, off-site key, query) are all near-zero here — the cleanest specificity ladder in this folder for a *functional interface* claim. Any mechanism our white-box model records should state its radius and its depth, and should name which of the three functions each of its units serves. (2) *Stage availability is not composition.* Table IV is the shape our model's predictive claims must survive: five training routes that all end up with the units in place and all fail unseen chains at chance, distinguished from the aligned route only by a pair-swap delta. Predictively: if a reading of a network's multi-hop behaviour cannot say whether the intermediate is actually fed forward to the next query, it is not a reading of the mechanism but of the network's component inventory. We should require a bridge test — patch the intermediate, check whether the endpoint moves. (3) *A hard, derived horizon.* `R_local ≤ L(W-1)` is a prediction from the architecture alone that is then confirmed at its own boundary (lag 3 alive, lag 4 exactly zero at W=2), which is the one place in this folder where a white-box model states in advance which context a component cannot see and then measures the boundary. Copy the pattern: derive the reach of each sub-part from its wiring, and treat a positive effect past that bound as a signal that the derivation's assumptions (no global attention, no recurrent state) are wrong rather than as a win. Two warnings: behavioural recall does not identify the route (the state-readout shift at strong retention produced high recall with no cross-layer circuit at all), and the trained-instance lookup attenuation in Proposition 4 is the formal version of "the model learned the table, not the rule" — a mechanism record scored on fixed instances should be re-scored on relabelled ones.

# SFT Leaves a Reproducible Signature in Activation Geometry: Calibrating Post-Training Interpretability Against Run-to-Run Noise

## What they wanted, and why

Every "model diff" in interpretability credits post-training with whatever separates a base checkpoint from one fine-tuned descendant. That inference silently assumes a rerun of the same recipe would land close to the first run — so the base-to-post gap is read as an effect rather than as noise. The authors ask the prior question: how much of a difference would you have to see before it exceeds what a second run of the same recipe produces on its own? They want a calibration protocol for sparse-dictionary model diffing, and they use it to ask which post-training stages actually leave a reproducible mark in activation space. The larger goal is a warning discipline for the whole "diff two checkpoints, name the features that changed" genre.

## What they studied

Qwen3-1.7B as the primary backbone, post-trained on BFCL function calling: four LoRA-SFT runs (rank 16, α 32 on attention projections, ~0.37% of parameters, 29 steps), four LoRA-DPO runs at 12 steps plus four retrained DPO runs read at 29 and 60 steps, and six full-parameter GRPO-family runs (an accuracy-only baseline, four further seeds, one shaped-reward variant at λ=0.1, checkpointed between 100 and 300 steps). A second backbone — Llama-3.1-8B with four LoRA-SFT runs — plus the public Tulu-3 staircase (Llama-3.1-8B base → SFT → DPO → RLVR-PPO, with Tulu-3.1-8B as a second RLVR run branched from the same DPO parent) complete the roster. The unit of analysis is the sparse co-activation graph over SAE features: 10,000 edges between source layer 14 and destination layer 22 of a dictionary trained on the base model only (qwen-scope, TopK k=50, d=32,768), Llama-Scope for the Llama arm. Because one base-trained basis serves every checkpoint, graphs are subsets of the same index set and so are directly comparable.

## How they did it

For each checkpoint M, a fixed probe set of 500 Pile prompts is run through the model; the residual states at layer 14 and layer 22 are encoded by the base-trained SAEs; edge weights are mean co-activation products over states, keeping the top 2,000 products per state and the top 10,000 globally. Similarity between two checkpoints is edge Jaccard; displacement is one minus that. The reference band is built from same-recipe reruns: for k runs, the k contrasts to base are scored against the m=k(k−1)/2 run-to-run pairs, summarized as a mean and SD, with a z-score and a simpler count of contrasts that fall below every seed pair. A precision rule gates which statistic may be scored — the seed SD must be at least ten times the reporting step of 0.0001 — which keeps edge Jaccard and demotes the weighted edge cosine to descriptive status on trained dictionaries. A build rule follows: graphs rebuild bit-for-bit on the architecture that produced them but not across architectures, where a rebuild moves edge Jaccard by roughly one seed-pair distance, so every band and its contrasts are scored on one GPU. Three controls are run against every level statistic: a matched base checkpoint (attribute only the base-to-post delta, never a single-checkpoint level), a degree-preserving permutation null (concentrated versus chance), and a random-direction encoder at matched width and sparsity. Finally a pre-registered causal sweep at three edge budgets on 4,816 held-out prompts corrupts the sources unique to the SFT graph and compares the output shift against frequency-matched random sources and against base-specific sources.

## What counts as an explanation here

A calibrated structural diff: a named set of feature-to-feature edges that moved further than the recipe's own reruns, plus a localization of where the movement lands (which destination hub, which source features). It is explicitly partial and explicitly non-causal — the authors retire the word "rewiring" and claim calibrated structural displacement, not a changed mechanism, because their own registered sweep says the stage-specificity interaction does not hold at two of three budgets. The deliverable is less a picture of what SFT did than a scale on which any such picture can be judged.

## How they judged success

Edge Jaccard between co-activation graphs; a run-to-run band with a standardized score and an outside-the-band count; Proposition 1, which bounds what band exceedance can certify under run exchangeability (at best 1/(k+1) when all k contrasts are outside, whatever the effect size, so z is a descriptive scale and not a tail probability); the precision rule; the one-architecture build rule; a random-dictionary control; edge-budget sweeps at 2,000 and 5,000 edges; a pre-registered replication on Llama-3.1-8B; an activation-side battery of narrowing metrics (firing-frequency Gini, mean-activation Gini, usage entropy, mean L0) with mean L0 excluded where the dictionary pins it; a minimum detectable effect calculation for the on-task arm; and the functional-transfer sweep with a faithfulness gate and a label-permutation p-value.

## What they found

SFT is detected against its own reruns: base-to-SFT sits 17.8 pairwise-distance SDs beyond the mean distance between four independent LoRA-SFT runs, and all five SFT contrasts fall outside the band. It replicates on Llama-3.1-8B, where all four contrasts sit beyond that band with the nearest at 1.7 times the band maximum. At 29 steps the four SFT and four DPO runs occupy disjoint displacement ranges (0.0571–0.0615 against 0.0317–0.0356 on one architecture, a gap several times either arm's spread), which needs no band at all; a two-sided permutation reading of that disjointness is at most 1/35, computed after the fact. DPO's band counts come out 3/4, 1/4, 3/4 outside at 12, 29 and 60 steps while displacement stays flat, so no dose ordering is claimed and no single z is promoted; the authors explicitly withdraw an earlier dose-dependent reading of 0/4, 2/4, 4/4 that turned out to lie within the rebuild difference. Light GRPO is a non-detection, 0/5 contrasts outside its band, with no equivalence test run. The concentration statistic that earlier drafts read as an RL signature is already present in the zero-RL base: base Gini 0.728 and top-1%-active 0.427 against GRPO's 0.730 and 0.429, with 31–32 of the 32 top hubs shared. Two additional artifacts are documented as self-corrections: mean L0 is pinned at exactly 50.0 on every Qwen checkpoint by the TopK dictionary and therefore carries no information, and an apparent "scaling law" in concentration vanished once the top-1%-of-dictionary denominator was replaced by a width-independent one (the honest 1.7B-to-8B pair is 0.7110 to 0.8395, and the residual rise is mechanical, 655 features against 328). The displaced edges are localized: of the edges base and all six GRPO runs keep but SFT drops, 63% of dropped weight feeds a single destination — the base graph's largest hub, a generic numeric/whitespace/markup feature — and the edges SFT adds are diffuse. The causal sweep nonetheless found the SFT-specific sources load-bearing against a random control (12 times in mean, 3.5 times in median at 10,000 edges) while the interaction relative to base-specific sources reverses at two of three budgets and the third fails its faithfulness gate, so the registered verdict is MIXED. At production scale on Tulu-3 the levels again stay near base and SFT plus DPO write most of the cumulative displacement, while no RLVR increment is resolved above a single run-pair reference that could certify at best p ≤ 1/3.

## Limits and open problems they name

The decisive light-RL control is one 1.7B backbone; the only cross-family post checkpoint is gemma-2-2b-it, which is heavy production instruction tuning and also possibly confounded by chat-template distribution mismatch, so it bounds heavy post-training rather than light GRPO. Long-horizon math-RLVR at larger scale sits entirely outside the envelope. The top-32 panel is H1-selected rather than random. Cross-tokenizer alignment has its own noise floor. The seed-noise band conflates seed, checkpoint step and reward recipe, which widens it — conservative for detection but anti-conservative for certifying the RL null — and the matched same-recipe, same-step remedy spans only three runs and three dependent contrasts, so it supports a descriptive consistency and not an equivalence claim. The on-task BFCL arm is reported as uninformative rather than as a null: its minimum detectable effect is roughly three times SFT's actual pile-arm effect and the SFT positive control does not separate on it. An OLMo-2 staircase is blocked, not omitted, because no base-trained SAE weights are public. The DPO comparison cannot separate objective from learning rate and dropout, both of which differ between arms. The SFT behavioral gain is not resolved from zero at n=100, and the behavioral and internal arms sit on different state distributions, so no dissociation is drawn. Pre/post results after publication include two withdrawn claims (an entropy-widening dimension that does not reproduce, and a steering gate whose corrected analysis clears 0/19).

## What we take from it

A **measure**, and the single most directly reusable one in this folder: the same-recipe rerun band. Every claim of the form "this checkpoint's features differ from that checkpoint's" can and should be scored against k reruns of the recipe, with the discipline that the band belongs to its own recipe — borrowing another stage's band is the substitution error this paper exists to catch. A **warning** we should apply to ourselves: a single-checkpoint concentration statistic is a level, not a contrast, and here the level was fully present in the zero-RL base with z=20 and stability across a 23× width sweep, so significance and scale-stability are no defence against the missing-base-checkpoint artifact. A **method** worth borrowing wholesale: the precision rule (check the statistic can move at all before scoring it — the TopK L0 result is a null that is really a constant) and the build rule (record which hardware produced the graph; a cross-architecture rebuild can move the statistic by a whole seed-pair distance and manufacture a dose curve). A **framing** for our prediction goal: the permutation rank floor is the honest ceiling on what k runs can certify, so a band-exceedance number should be reported as a scale with a stated k, never as a p-value. And a **floor** on the claim that post-training leaves structural traces that distinguish stages: at equal step count SFT and DPO separate cleanly without any band, light RL does not separate from its own reruns, and the SFT signature localizes to boilerplate-to-hub edges — which argues that the probe direction we read after fine-tuning may be measuring a level the base already had unless it is differenced against both a matched base and a rerun.

# Breaking Refusal in the First Half: A Mechanistic Study of the Prefill Jailbreak

- **Key:** arxiv-2607.14147
- **Authors / organisation:** Alex Kwon (Independent Researcher)
- **Date:** 2026-07-14
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14147
- **Kind:** paper
- **Read from:** full text
- **Relevance to us:** 3 (central) — the folder's cleanest case of a representation that is intact, decodable and causally upstream, yet not the thing that decides the behaviour

## What they wanted, and why
Alignment training teaches a model to refuse harmful requests, and a single affirmative line forced into the start of its own reply dissolves that refusal. Prior work established the behaviour — safety looks shallow in token depth, and forced-prefix attacks work — but not where the decision actually fails. The author asks a strictly mechanistic question underneath it: the prefill is appended to the response, so it cannot touch the representation the model built over the prompt; does the model build a strong harm representation there at all at the moment it complies, and if so, what does the refusal gate compute over? The larger goal is a defence that is placed correctly rather than one that is trained harder, plus a test of whether "refusal" is a feature, a direction, a circuit, or a positional computation — the four things the interpretability literature usually offers.

## What they studied
Four instruction-tuned models across three families — Qwen2.5-1.5B and 3B, SmolLM2-1.7B, Phi-3-mini-3.8B — with Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen2.5-7B-base and Phi-3-medium-14B as scale and safety-training checks, all on CPU except the 7B and 14B runs. Units of analysis span the whole ladder: a linear harm probe at a single position, the residual stream at response positions and layers, subspaces and single directions inside it, attention edges (query–key pairs), attention heads, whole attention layers, and the early MLP sub-block. Prompts are AdvBench behaviours as harmful, Alpaca as benign, XSTest for the surface-versus-harm control, plus a 24-triplet dual-use intent set.

## How they did it
The attack forces the assistant turn to open with an affirmative prefix and lets generation continue; refusal is scored by matching a fixed refusal-phrase list, and every claimed effect is additionally read by a judge-free coherence-and-content rule so "non-refusal" can be told apart from deflection. The harm probe is an L2 logistic regression on the hidden state at the last prompt token, fitted harmful-versus-benign, with nested cross-validation for layer selection. For the causal work they sweep activation patching across layers and injection strengths, then settle two cells chosen from the grid in advance at n = 100 against a matched-norm direction orthogonalised to the harm direction over 24 random seeds, with bootstrap CIs; a held-out replication retrains the direction on a disjoint 200-prompt split. A dose-matched position control holds per-position strength constant while varying *where* the direction is injected across the prefill-plus-18-token response window, which separates position from dose. The converse arm estimates the model's own refuse-versus-comply state shift as a difference of mean residuals over the early generated window, trains it on 260 prompts and injects it on a disjoint 100. An attention-edge knockout adds a negative bias to the pre-softmax logits of every early-query-to-prefill-key edge and lets softmax renormalise, with a per-prompt mass-matched control on non-prefill keys (achieved ratio 0.98) and a unit-test suite — bit-identity of a sham hook, edge-only masking, KV-cache consistency — passed before any behavioural run. A base model with no safety tuning serves as the discriminator between active suppression and passive conditioning, and a frozen-residual path patch routes only the early-MLP pathway with the same unit-test discipline. A pre-registered 2x2 crosses harm with trigger-word density using natural variation in existing prompt sets, with the keyword lexicon and labels fixed before outcomes were read.

## What counts as an explanation here
A positional causal account, plus a ledger of demotions. The explanation is that refusal is a shallow computation at the response site, not a property of the harm representation: the concept sits intact at the prompt, the action is severed from it in an early multi-token window of the response, and what maintains the severed state is generic early-window attention to the forced prefix. The form is a localisation — layer, position band, pathway — carried by interventions, not a dictionary or a circuit graph. It is explicitly partial: the author states the mechanism is diffuse and that the only compact failure point identified is positional rather than representational. It does predict: where a prompt-side monitor is immune (response-site attacks) and where it is not (prompt-modifying ones), and that a single harm direction will restore some refusal while over-refusing benign inputs.

## How they judged success
Restoration of refusal rate in percentage points against a matched-norm random direction; the same delta against a mass-matched attention control; paired bootstrap 95% CIs computed over prompts and seeds; a held-out split for every fitted direction; benign-sparing as a difference-in-differences; coherence (distinct-token ratio) reported in every condition; two independent scorers that must move in opposite directions (refusal rate up iff harmful content down); probe AUC under nested cross-validation with no test-set peeking; a pre-registered 2x2 with a logistic fit of refusal on harm and surface; and a controls ledger that classifies every check as passed, demoted, null or retracted.

## What they found
On plain prompts the harm probe separates harmful from benign at AUC 0.996 on Qwen2.5-1.5B and at least 0.990 on all four models. Under the prefill, refusal on Qwen2.5-1.5B falls from 1.00 to 0.03 while the complied prompts score 0.975 on the harm probe against 0.966 for the ones it refuses and 0.055 for benign — but the author is careful that this half is structural, since the probed position precedes any attack token. The substantive part is that where a weakened prefill leaves a real split (41 refuse versus 29 comply of 70), harm strength does predict who complies, at AUC 0.63 to 0.72, and still 0.73 after regressing out prompt length and scary-keyword count. Full-residual patching restores refusal to 100% but so does patching the mean *benign* residual, which demotes it to generic disruption. What survives is the subspace rung: at layer 12 the harm direction restores 48% against 14% for the orthogonal random control (+34 pp, CI +22 to +46), at layer 8 33% against 16% (+17 pp, CI +6 to +28), benign-sparing, and a held-out retrain gives 42% against 13% (+29 pp). The position control finds the first half of the response restores 42% as against 41% for the whole, while the second half gives 6% at double strength and the onset token 9%; excluding the prefill span drops restoration to at most 3%, so the write-site inside the early window is the prefill itself, and a long committal prefix is far harder to reverse (6–12%) than a short one (86%). Injecting the model's own refuse-state restores refusal from near zero to 74% on the held-out split, as much as the whole response (78%), against 0% for the matched-norm random vector, with a +57 pp selectivity gap over benign. Knocking out early response-to-prefill attention restores refusal from 3% to 24% against 1% for the mass-matched control (+21 pp, CI +12 to +30), early-window-gated, harm-selective (benign DiD +16.8 pp), taking the harmful-content rate from 51% to 18%. The base-model control is the paper's pivot: on the untuned Qwen2.5-1.5B base the identical knockout collapses harmful content 64% to 25% against the matched control's 64% (+39 pp), replicated at 7B (68% to 30%, +36 pp), so the grip is generic autoregressive conditioning and refusal is a model-dependent fallback. A small safety-specific attractor survives on top — the instruct model routes knockout-freed probability mass to refusal tokens about 8x more than the base, concentration 0.24 against 0.03. The stolen-signal test that sealed it: a difference-of-means injection cannot distinguish decodability from active computation, but a frozen-residual path patch through the early MLP strips prefill-specific compliance in both models (−21 and −25 pp against matched −7 and −5) with no safety-specific refusal pull at all (concentration 0.007 versus base 0.001). On the gate's readout, the pre-registered 2x2 shows refusal is harm-primary — stripping trigger words leaves refusal at 100% and the probe at 0.982 against 0.983, with logistic coefficients 5.12 for harm and 2.20 for surface — and the keyword effect is real but secondary, appearing as over-refusal on benign scary-sounding prompts (0.59 against 0.08), whose magnitude is an upper bound because that cell is entirely XSTest. A search for a separate refusal-decision direction finds none: the diff-of-means direction is nearly orthogonal to harm (absolute cosine 0.06) and reads the decision at only 0.648 against harm's own 0.640, any single direction ceilings at 0.727, a full linear probe reads 0.85, and steering that direction is inert wherever it is applied. Across the Qwen ladder the full probe beats the best single direction at every scale (0.763 to 0.843 at 0.5B, 0.727 to 0.848 at 1.5B, 0.847 to 0.942 at 7B) while SmolLM2 shows no gain at all (0.829 single against 0.805 full). Finally, no intervention selectively removes refusal: every one of the 28x12 head ablations drops refusal by 0.00 with the hook verified to move logits, and heavier ablation only collapses generation into a fixed gibberish string.

## Limits and open problems they name
The active-versus-passive question is resolved by the base-model discriminator toward passive, and the author is explicit that the resolution rests on a teacher-forced path patch that is better powered for compliance-suppression routing than for a small refusal-promotion component. Small models, CPU-first, with the full battery on two of them; frontier scale untested. The harm probe is AdvBench-calibrated — it fires at 0.97 on that phrasing and 0.49 on differently-worded unsafe prompts — and the mixed-source repair wrecked specificity, so a source-general monitor is unbuilt and the 100%-catch figure is an in-distribution number. The monitor's immunity is scoped to response-site attacks; prompt-modifying attacks attenuate or displace the representation instead. The single-versus-full comparison is Qwen-scoped with one balanced cross-family counterexample. MLP neurons and sparse autoencoder features were not probed and could still host a compact mechanism. Measurement uses a refusal-phrase string match rather than a judge.

## What we take from it
Three things, one of them a method we should copy outright. (1) **A demotion ledger as the deliverable.** The paper's Table 5 and Appendix L list every load-bearing claim beside the control that could have killed it and a verdict — passed, demoted, null, retracted — and the author states that about a third of the verdicts are not "passed." This is the shape a predictive white-box model's report should take: not a list of effects but a table of which ones survived which rival. (2) **The base-model discriminator.** The single best instrument here for separating "the model learned a safety-specific mechanism" from "the architecture does this anyway" is to run the identical intervention on an untrained-for-the-task model of the same family. Our model should carry a base or randomly-initialised arm on every mechanism claim, because here it converted a plausible active-suppression story into a passive-conditioning one with two numbers. (3) **Read-out is not write-handle, and the site is the variable.** The same direction injected at response positions restores refusal selectively (48% harmful, 12% benign) while injected at all positions restores more but drags benign to 40% — the difference is site, not vector. Warning to carry: the dramatic version of every result in this paper was the wrong one, and each dramatic version had a control that slew it (benign-residual patch for full-residual restoration, benign non-affirmative prefix for the onset projection, decorrelated 2x2 for the "separate feature"). Framing worth borrowing: **a concept can be fully represented, decodable, causally upstream, and still not be the gate** — so a probe that reads X at 0.98 tells us what the model knows, and a separate positional experiment is required to learn where the model acts on it.

## What they wanted, and why

The paper's premise is that a list of separately-studied training phenomena - sharp attention concentration ("bubbling"), grokking-style delayed generalisation, circuit formation, tolerance of noisy optimisation - may all be symptoms of one geometric fact, and it sets out to find that fact. The hypothesis is that in an over-parameterised transformer the weight trajectory does not wander in the full parameter space but falls rapidly onto a very low-dimensional surface, which the author calls the *execution manifold*, and that most of the remaining dimensions serve only to absorb optimisation noise. If true, this would be a re-framing of what interpretability should look for: not features in activations of a finished model, but the small subspace in which the learning path lives. The stated larger goal is interpretability at scale - if the computational core is low-dimensional, you need not confront an exponentially large parameter space - and the paper is candid that its evidence comes from one synthetic task family.

## What they studied

Attention-only and standard (attention + MLP) Transformers, all with embedding dimension d = 128, so each attention matrix has 16,384 entries and the analysed parameter vector is of order 10^4-10^5. The task is marker-based modular addition: sequences of length 32 containing 1-6 non-adjacent marker tokens, each followed by a value in 0..C-1, with the target being the sum of marked values mod C (C = 8 or 16), surrounded by distractor tokens. Difficulty is varied through the marker count and through curriculum (mixed simultaneous training versus progressive increase of the marker count). Training is plain SGD at lr 1e-3, batch 64, checkpoints every 100 steps, 20 seeds per condition. The unit of analysis is a **subspace of parameter space** - the leading principal components of each attention matrix's trajectory - plus **per-matrix effective rank** and a **commutator vector** built from pairs of mini-batch gradients.

## How they did it

Three measurements are layered. (1) **Trajectory PCA**: snapshots of each attention matrix are flattened to vectors in R^{16384}, a covariance matrix is formed over checkpoints, and the effective rank r90 is the smallest number of components reaching 90% of trajectory variance; the top-K components are then embedded at their offsets in the full parameter vector and QR-orthonormalised to give an orthonormal basis B for the "execution subspace". (2) **Commutator defect**: two independent mini-batches A and B are applied in both orders with a fixed step size, and the resulting parameter difference theta_AB - theta_BA is the non-commutativity of the two update directions; it is reported both raw and normalised by the product of the two step norms, on the argument that to first order this difference is the Lie bracket of the gradient fields, i.e. a Hessian-free local curvature probe. (3) **Projection with a random control**: the commutator is projected onto B (fraction rho_exec) and onto ten freshly drawn random bases of the same rank (fraction rho_rand), and the ratio rho_exec/rho_rand is treated as the real diagnostic, on the explicit reasoning that a K-dimensional subspace of a P-dimensional space captures roughly sqrt(K/P) of any vector and so a raw fraction proves nothing. Two further strands are attached: attention entropy over training is tracked as a continuous proxy for bubbling, and sparse autoencoders (ReLU encoder, L1 penalty, decoder) are trained on intermediate activations and then ablated latent-by-latent to test whether any of them carries the computation. A curriculum comparison and a preliminary modular-multiplication run probe robustness.

## What counts as an explanation here

A **geometric and dynamical-systems account**, with the explanation object being a subspace plus a small set of scalars attached to it: the intrinsic dimension r90, the projection fractions rho_exec and rho_rand, their ratio, and the attention entropy curve. Nothing is described in text terms and no circuit is recovered; the claim is that three previously separate phenomena are *projections of one geometric object* - bubbling is saturation along a routing coordinate inside the manifold, grokking is the discovery of an integrable low-dimensional subspace, and SAE features are peripheral bookkeeping rather than computation. It is explicitly partial and, in the author's own framing, a *framework* rather than a prediction engine: no quantity here is offered as forecasting a specific behaviour at a specific step, and the one forward-looking question the paper raises (can early geometry predict eventual generalisation) is listed as future work, not answered.

## How they judged success

Four checks, of very different strength. (i) **Stability across seeds** - the effective rank is asserted to be 3-4 across 20 seeds while the subspace *orientation* varies, which the author reads as evidence that the object is intrinsic rather than coordinate-dependent. (ii) **The matched random-subspace control** - rho_exec against rho_rand averaged over ten random bases, with the ratio reported at 9.7x median in the first 20% of training and 2.1x in the last 20%, and a peak of about 14x in one panel. (iii) **The perpendicular fraction** - the share of commutator norm orthogonal to the execution subspace, quoted as above 92% throughout and above 98% late. (iv) **Ablation of SAE latents** - accuracy before and after zeroing task-correlated latents at three training stages. There is no held-out prediction test, no tabulated per-seed table, no error bars on the projection ratios, and - importantly - **no baseline for the effective-rank number itself**, i.e. no random-walk or untrained-model null for r90, which is the one statistic the paper's headline rests on. Figures 1-6 carry the evidence visually; apart from the handful of ratios in the prose, nothing is tabulated.

## What they found

The headline is a collapse from a 16,384-dimensional parameter space per matrix to an execution manifold of dimension 3-4, reached within the first 20-30% of training, with all four attention matrices landing between 3 and 5 and W_V/W_O consistently lower than W_Q/W_K. The dimension is reported as stable for marker counts up to 6 and to rise only slightly (4-6) for modular multiplication. Commutator structure is the paper's one genuinely comparative result: the execution basis captures 9.7x (median) more commutator energy than a rank-matched random basis early in training, decaying to 2.1x late, while the orthogonal complement holds more than 92% of commutator magnitude throughout and more than 98% late; the author reads this as non-commutativity being progressively expelled from the computational directions into a "staging" buffer. Attention entropy falls smoothly with no discontinuity, offered as evidence that bubbling is saturation rather than a discrete transition. SAE latents: only 3-5 of several hundred correlate with task structure (marker count, marked positions) and zeroing them barely moves accuracy at any stage. Two negative results are reported plainly: adding MLPs destroys the clean 3-4-dimensional picture with no clear collapse and worse generalisation under the same budget, and progressive curriculum training causes catastrophic forgetting of the small-marker cases even though mixed training shows the capacity exists.

## Limits and open problems they name

The author's own list is unusually honest and matches what a reader would object to anyway: the task is synthetic, linear and possibly over-friendly to low-dimensional solutions; nothing here tests a naturalistic model, and the scaling claim - that bigger models on harder tasks also collapse - is explicitly not tested. The MLP result is presented as a tension between expressivity and geometric simplicity without any account of which is doing what. The paper's own list of future work names exactly the missing pieces: a scaling law for execution dimension, early-training diagnostics as predictors, nonlinear measures of intrinsic dimension for tasks without global linear structure, and how micro-circuits compose. Beyond the named limits, one methodological gap is worth flagging for us: r90 at a fixed 90% threshold is a monotone function of how much variance the trajectory's first component already holds, so a trajectory that moves almost entirely along one direction and a trajectory that moves along four well-separated ones can both report small ranks for reasons unrelated to computation - and the paper never reports the variance spectrum or a null. Nothing is said about how many checkpoints the PCA uses, which determines the attainable rank directly.

## What we take from it

A **framing plus one reusable instrument, and one warning**. The reusable instrument is the one this paper does well: rho_exec against rho_rand, reported as a ratio with the raw fraction printed beside it, and the *decay of that ratio over training* read as the substantive finding rather than its peak. That ratio curve - 9.7x early, 2.1x late - is exactly the shape runs 717 and 722 taught us to look for, and here it is the author's own headline rather than something a reader has to reconstruct. The warning is sharper: **an effective-rank number at a fixed variance threshold, with no spectrum printed and no null, is not a measurement of computation** - it is a measurement of how many trailing eigenvalues the 90% cut happens to retain, which depends on the number of checkpoints, the step size and whether the model moved at all; before we report any "the model computes in k dimensions" claim we print the eigenvalue spectrum, the checkpoint count and the same statistic for an untrained model or a random walk of matched length. The framing worth keeping is the buffer idea - most non-commutativity confined to directions orthogonal to the path - because it makes a falsifiable prediction we can test cheaply: if staging directions really absorb interference, perturbing along them should be behaviourally inert while perturbing inside the manifold should hurt, and this paper never runs that intervention. Note also the SAE result, which is the folder's third independent sighting of "a feature that is decodable and causally empty": rank-3-5 latents that track marker counts and, when zeroed, move nothing.

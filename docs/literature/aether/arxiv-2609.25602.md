# Rewired or Gated? How Instruction Tuning Shapes Knowledge-Conflict Circuits in LLMs

- **Key:** arxiv-2609.25602
- **Authors / organisation:** Shubham Pandere, Gautam Ranka, Ritika Varshney, Navya Deshmukh, Roushni Sareen, Roshan Kumar Singh; IvLabs, VNIT
- **Date:** 2026-09
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25602
- **Kind:** paper
- **Read from:** full text (arXiv HTML v1, 286 math nodes recovered via alttext substitution; Tables 1–10 read cell by cell; figures 1–6 read from captions and reporting prose only)
- **Relevance to us:** 3 (central)

## What they wanted, and why
When a prompt asserts something the model's weights say is false, the model has to arbitrate between context and memory. Prior mechanistic work had localised that arbitration to a small set of late attention heads. Separately, work on how fine-tuning changes circuits had reached two different answers - one task showing enhancement of an existing circuit, another showing stable nodes but shifted edges. Nobody had looked at knowledge conflict, where the tuning objective and the task interact most directly, so this paper asks the binary question: does instruction tuning **rewire** the conflict circuit (new heads become critical) or merely **gate** it (the same heads get reweighted)? The motivation is practical: if tuning only gates, then steering vectors, pruning recipes and probes calibrated on a freely released base checkpoint should carry over to the deployed instruct sibling without being rediscovered.

## What they studied
Three base/instruct pairs: Llama-3.2-3B, Qwen-2.5-3B, Gemma-3-4B. No training is performed; only inference-time behaviour on released weights. Data is a factual set derived from ParaConflict, filtered so both the memory answer and the distinct distractor are single tokens under each model's own tokenizer (764 surviving rows for Llama and Qwen, an identical set; 870 for Gemma, a superset; 2,146 before filtering from four relation domains - athlete–sport, official language, company headquarters, world capital). The unit of analysis is the (layer, head) node, the edge between nodes, and the per-head memory-vs-context role.

## How they did it
Each fact is delivered under two conflict forms. *Substitution* inserts a terse false clause and restates the stem; *coherent* expands the same falsehood into a multi-sentence evidential passage that repeats the distractor and closes with a question/answer turn. Because both answers are single tokens, every swap is length-preserving, which lets the attribution counterfactual be an in-place replacement of each distractor occurrence with the memory target and keeps both sequences aligned position by position. Four quantities are measured: **Contextual Reliance Rate** (CRR - the fraction of prompts where greedy generation follows the injected context), **node overlap** (Jaccard of top-k head sets plus Spearman/Kendall of full rankings), **edge overlap** (Jaccard of top-k EAP-IG edges over an 87k–390k edge graph with 5 integration steps), and a **per-head superposition index** built from difference-in-log-attributions context versus memory pull, scaled to [-1, 1]. Node importance uses logit-derivative saliency against the context-minus-memory logit contrast. Two validation passes check the cheap screen: PH3-style path patching on the top 20 heads, and causal ablation that amplifies (scales up), mean-ablates, or zero-ablates the selected heads and re-measures CRR **against a sign predicted before the run**. The paper ties the axes together with a pre-registered decision table (Table 1): node overlap decides gating versus rewiring, edge overlap only sub-types a gating verdict, and a large CRR shift rules out the null.

## What counts as an explanation here
A three-axis verdict about a circuit's fate under one specific fine-tuning step: *which nodes* carry the behaviour, *how they are wired*, and *where they sit*. The explanation is comparative and structural rather than a description of a mechanism - the claim "gating, not rewiring" is a statement about conservation of an identified set and its ranking, and it is meant to predict that a control tool built on the base model still has a live target in the instruct model. It is partial by construction: it says what survives, not what the surviving heads compute, and the edge axis is explicitly confined to one conflict form.

## How they judged success
Pre-registered overlap bands (high at or above 0.6, rewired below 0.3) so that the verdict is not chosen after seeing the number; a hypergeometric permutation null for every overlap (random top-k sets out of the L×H head universe); a J@k cut-off sensitivity table; a second set-based metric (rank-biased overlap at p=0.9) and Kendall's tau-b on the full rankings; three saliency flavours as a robustness check; a **size- and layer-matched random-head ablation floor** (10 random 5-head sets drawn from the same upper-half layers, excluding the selected heads); a 36-cell sign-match test against a-priori predictions; a continuous log-probability variant of CRR as a check against argmax near-ties; and an abstention bucket so "followed neither" is not silently scored as "followed memory".

## What they found
**The behavioural shift is real, large, and in the opposite direction to the naive expectation.** Under terse substitution CRR falls in every family: Llama 0.675 → 0.263, Qwen 0.645 → 0.220, Gemma 0.870 → 0.369 (all p < 1e-15, z = 16–22). The log-probability variant agrees within 2.6 points per cell. **The shift is a framing effect.** Under the coherent passage both variants follow the false context near ceiling (0.88–1.00) and the gaps shrink to −0.029 / −0.122 / −0.005. Abstention rises too (Gemma goes from 0 to 48 "neither" prompts). So tuning buys resistance to terse unsupported contradiction, not to well-written misinformation.

**Gating holds on every axis.** Top-head Jaccard J@10 ranges 0.67–0.82 and the importance ranking correlates at Spearman 0.82–0.91 (on absolute score; signed-score Spearman 0.65/0.84/0.77 for Qwen/Llama/Gemma); an independent superposition-pipeline cross-check gives 0.74 / 0.60 / 0.82. Every overlap clears its hypergeometric null (p ≤ 7e-15, z = 24–40). Roles are stable: only 2–3 heads per family flip context/memory role, and mean index drift is near zero and slightly memory-ward (−0.009, +0.003, −0.046). Edges sub-type rather than overturn: Llama is strict gating (edge J@50 = 0.613), Qwen 0.389 and Gemma 0.539 land in a partial band - the direction the "nodes stable, edges shift" literature predicted - but neither reaches the 0.3 rewiring threshold. The full-edge ranking still correlates at Spearman 0.69–0.72. **Causation matches prediction in all 36 cells** (6 model variants × 6 conditions; binomial p ≈ 2^-36). The circuit is asymmetric: suppressing the five top context heads collapses context-following in the base models (ΔCRR Gemma −0.83, Qwen −0.41, Llama −0.31) while the five memory heads move CRR far less (+0.04 to +0.25), and effects attenuate in instruct models as a floor effect. **The random-head floor is the key specificity result:** ten random 5-head sets from the same upper-half layers move CRR by −0.03 to +0.02 on average, against −0.10 to −0.83 for the selected context heads - the selected effect exceeds the most extreme random set in all six variants for context suppression and in four of six for memory suppression (the exceptions being Llama, whose memory effect is small in absolute terms). **Localisation is preserved:** the shared heads are 81–100% in the upper half of the network and median conflict-head depth moves by at most two layers (24→22, 32→31, 28→28 in the 28/36/34-layer models); over 95% of the importance of answer-feeding edges sits in upper layers in both variants. Independently assigned superposition roles agree with the LDS context/memory direction on 55 of 60 head assignments, with all five misses being weak-index heads near the role threshold.

## Limits and open problems they name
Scale: everything is 3–4B, and whether gating survives at larger scale is untested. **Edges are measured only on the substitution form** - the coherent passage repeats and reframes the distractor, which breaks the common-prefix/suffix window EAP-IG aligns on - so C2a has a node-level but no edge-level counterpart. The single-token filter removes whole relations (book–author and company–founder drop out entirely) and rules out multi-token and free-generation following. One corpus, four domains, no held-out natural conflict set, and no cross-model activation-patching guard for the two ambiguous edge sub-types. Path-patching per-cell p-values are individually insignificant (0.10–0.42); confidence in that leg comes from six-cell consistency, and the authors label it corroborative rather than confirming. They also concede that four of their five views are observational and that path patching and ablation target LDS-selected heads, so membership overlap with LDS is not independent evidence.

## What we take from it
- **A method: pre-register the bands, not just the metric.** Writing 0.6 / 0.3 down before seeing the overlap is what stops a three-axis comparison from being read as whichever axis happened to win. Take the table itself (Table 1) as a template - the rule that a node-level verdict cannot be overturned by an edge-level result, only sub-typed by it, is a piece of decision architecture worth reusing verbatim in our own evaluation.
- **A measure: the size- and layer-matched random floor as a standing control.** "The selected heads matter" and "perturbing upper-layer attention matters" are separated by ten random 5-head sets from the same layers; the floor lands at essentially zero while the selected set lands at −0.31 to −0.83. Any selection rule we publish gets this control.
- **A measure: cut-off sensitivity in one table.** Edge J@10 would have called all three families "high" and J@1000 would have called all three "low"; only the pre-fixed J@50 is operative. This is the folder's existing rule about naming an exhaustive limit arriving as a simple column sweep.
- **A warning: Jaccard overlaps between head sets are not comparable across cut-offs or across metrics, and a single cut-off can carry a verdict.** RBO exposes that Qwen's very top edges drift (0.53) while its J@50 was merely "partial" - one number was hiding a nuance the other showed.
- **A framing: "the model became more robust" is a claim about one phrasing.** A factor-of-nearly-two CRR drop under terse injection is compatible with a ceiling-level CRR under a fluent passage. Framing is a confound of the same order as the effect, and the two-form design is the cheap way to find that out.
- **A prediction we can test on ourselves:** if base and instruct sit 0.6–0.8 apart in head identity while behaviour moves by half the scale, then a white-box model fitted on the base checkpoint should predict the instruct sibling's conflict behaviour with a *memory-ward gain adjustment and no structural change*. That is a concrete, falsifiable transfer claim our own model can be scored against.

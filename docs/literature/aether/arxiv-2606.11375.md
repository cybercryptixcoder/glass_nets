# When Probing Accuracy Saturates, Fragility Resolves: A Complementary Metric for LLM Pre-Training Analysis

- **Key:** arxiv-2606.11375
- **Authors / organisation:** Orion Reblitz-Richardson; Distiller Labs (sole author)
- **Date:** 2026-06-09 (v1); revised 2026-08-25 (v2, the version read)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11375
- **Kind:** paper
- **Read from:** full text
- **Relevance to us:** 2 (useful)

## What they wanted, and why
Linear probing is the field's default way of saying a property is "encoded": train a classifier on frozen hidden states, report accuracy per layer, call the property encoded wherever accuracy is high. The author's complaint is that this protocol answers a snapshot question and cannot answer a trajectory question. On the OLMo-2 1B early-training checkpoints, a binary probe on 240 moral/neutral minimal pairs is already at roughly 95% mean accuracy across all 16 layers by step 4K, and then returns essentially the same number for the remaining ~33K steps - about 95% of the sampled trajectory becomes invisible to the instrument. The goal is a complementary per-layer number that keeps moving after accuracy ceilings out, so that representational change during pre-training can be tracked rather than assumed. The paper also uses that number to make a substantive claim about how moralized representations are acquired, but the claim it wants judged is methodological: *where accuracy returns a flat answer, fragility returns a structured one*.

## What they studied
Two open-checkpoint OLMo models as the main subjects: OLMo-2 1B early-training (37 checkpoints at 1K-step intervals, steps 0-36K, ~76B tokens) and OLMo-3 7B stage 1 (probing on 20 checkpoints to ~1.4M steps / ~10T tokens, fragility on 5 of them), with OLMo-2 1B final (~2.2T tokens) used only for a validation gate. The unit of analysis is (layer, checkpoint) - a per-layer linear probe re-read at each training step - so the object being tracked is the geometry of the residual stream over time, not neurons or circuits. Four minimal-pair datasets define the read targets: a standard moral set (single morally-loaded lexeme swapped; 240 pairs, 40 per Moral Foundations Theory category, drawn from a 1,200-pair pool), sentiment (210 pairs, one valenced adjective), syntax (210 pairs, local grammaticality violations), and a hand-built compositional moral set (200 pairs in four 50-pair construction families - motive, target, consequence, role - where the action verb is held fixed and only individually-mild tokens vary, so valence resolves only from the surrounding action context). One author, no cluster: everything runs on a single MacBook Pro M4 Pro via MPS.

## How they did it
For each layer and checkpoint: mean-pool hidden states over the sequence, train a single linear layer with BCE loss, Adam at learning rate 1e-2, 50 epochs, no weight decay or early stopping, on a deterministic seed-42 split. Three summary statistics come out of the accuracy curve - onset layer (first layer at 0.6), peak layer, and encoding depth/breadth - and the cross-checkpoint onset step is read separately at a 0.70 threshold over the all-layer mean. Fragility is then measured on the same trained probe: add Gaussian noise at a given standard deviation to the cached test activations and re-evaluate, sweeping a logarithmic grid, and record the smallest noise level at which accuracy falls below 0.6 (chance plus 0.1 on a binary task). That critical noise is the paper's fragility number; layers that never cross the threshold are censored at the grid maximum rather than dropped. They run the same procedure on standard and compositional probes, on both model families. Separately they fine-tune LoRA adapters (rank 16, alpha 32, q_proj plus v_proj, lr 2e-4, 1000 steps) from the step-1000 checkpoint on three corpora of different character - narrative moral (fables), declarative moral (template-expanded sentences repeated), and a general non-moral control - and re-run the probe and the fragility test every 100 LoRA steps. The load-bearing control in v2 is a scale correction: per-layer activation scale changes with depth, so they re-run fragility with activations standardized to unit RMS (and, for the fine-tuning cell, with a closed-form analytic critical noise computed from the frozen probe's margin distribution). Within-layer comparisons are argued to be scale-free by construction; cross-layer ones are not.

## What counts as an explanation here
A scalar per layer per checkpoint: the activation-noise level at which a trained probe stops working. It is explicitly a probe-side functional of representation geometry, meant to be sensitive to two things at once - how wide the separation margin is, and how redundant the encoding is across directions - while, by the author's own admission, not separating the two contributions, and while also being movable by activation-scale changes, anisotropy, or probe-training instability. Nothing here predicts a network's behaviour under intervention; the claim is diagnostic resolution, not mechanism. The one causal instrument in the paper (Appendix B, a ROME-style causal tracer on 7B) is reported as an uncontrolled exploratory observation and explicitly not as support for the thesis, because the peak-layer argmax is unstable across probe seeds and the tracer has no specificity control.

## How they judged success
Four lines of evidence. (1) **Saturation is demonstrated, not asserted**: accuracy at ceiling by step 4K on a 37-checkpoint trajectory, with the flat stretch measured. (2) **Resolution after saturation**: fragility keeps moving across the same 32K steps, and the movement is replicated on a second probe family with four split seeds under a *pre-registered* decision rule (decline counts as real only if the 4-seed mean drops by at least 1.0 between two steps and the seed-to-seed standard deviation at both endpoints is smaller than the gap). (3) **A scale-controlled separation**: three fine-tuning conditions with identical probe accuracy are compared at matched layers on a shared base checkpoint, so scale is held fixed by construction, and the ordering is then re-derived with an analytic estimator and a paired bootstrap interval. (4) **A lexical floor for every probe**: a pair-disjoint, orientation-invariant unigram TF-IDF classifier gives the bag-of-words ceiling per dataset (0.86 standard moral, 0.80 sentiment, 0.63 compositional, 0.59 syntax), and the compositional claim is required to beat that floor and to transfer across construction families. Baselines are the trained probe at zero noise, the bag-of-words classifier, and the chance rate; the controls are 10 noise draws per cell, four split seeds, censoring handled explicitly, and an extended grid wherever censoring is heavy.

## What they found
The methodological result is real within its own frame. Standard moral probe accuracy goes 0.586 at step 0 to 0.942 by step 4K and sits at 0.953 at step 36K, while raw mean critical noise runs 0.52 → 18.31 (step 4K) → 18.44 (step 10K) → 4.69 (step 36K): it keeps moving through the whole post-saturation window. The compositional probe replicates on four seeds (0.10 at step 0, peak 5.11 at step 5K, 2.46 at step 30K), with the seed spread collapsing from 1.57 to 0.12 as training proceeds, and its decline passes the pre-registered rule with a realized gap of 2.19 against a maximum endpoint spread of 0.84. The curation result is the cleanest demonstration and the one the paper itself nominates as the demonstration: three LoRA conditions land within one point of each other on accuracy (0.740 / 0.750 / 0.750) but separate on fragility (mean critical noise 7.38 narrative, 5.63 declarative, 6.94 control; fragility dips at 10 of 16 layers under declarative training against 6 and 7 for the natural-text conditions), and the corpus with the deepest loss reduction is the most diffusely fragile.

The correction is the more important half. Per-layer activation RMS grows ≈0.13 → ≈1.0 going from early to late layers, roughly 8×, so a fixed absolute noise is a far larger relative perturbation near the input. Once noise is scaled to each layer's RMS, the late/early critical-noise ratio falls from 14.7× (step 10K) and 7.1× (step 36K) to a band near 2× (1.8-3.1×) and the ordering fails outright at 8 of 37 checkpoints; the post-saturation trajectory that looked like evolution flattens (13.8 to 15.0 across the sweep), and the same happens to the compositional probe (4.1 to 5.9). The v2 note states plainly that the v1 post-saturation decline is withdrawn as an activation-scale artifact and the layer-depth gradient is not claimed as genuine. What survives is within-layer comparison, and even there the magnitude shrinks: under the analytic scale-controlled estimator the declarative-versus-narrative gap is 2.90 against 4.11 and 4.63 raw, and 1.37 against 1.75 and 1.87 in signal-to-noise units, with the paired bootstrap interval excluding zero either way (raw 1.34, 95% CI [0.24, 2.47]; SNR 0.44, 95% CI [0.01, 0.91]) - the ordering robust, the magnitude about three times smaller and only marginally clearing zero.

On the staging claim, the standard probe onsets at step 1K, sentiment at 2K, compositional moral at 5K (per-seed 4K-7K) and syntax at 6K, but the author immediately undercuts the ordering as evidence: the compositional onset of 0.709 sits about 8 percentage points above its own 0.63 lexical floor, and onset ordering across all four datasets tracks lexical-floor height. The real compositional evidence is transfer and lift at the final checkpoint: leave-construction-out transfer 0.848 against 0.598 for bag-of-words, in-distribution 0.858 at layer 7, and lift of +0.20 to +0.28 above the per-construction floor, with the role-reversal family (lowest floor, 0.57) decoding at 0.85 and transferring at 0.81. Across training, transfer rises from chance (0.55) to plateau near 0.82 by step 9K and holds to step 36K (0.83).

## Limits and open problems they name
The one-cell-per-condition census is fragile: Appendix A's per-foundation table is sixteenths on a 16-example test, single seed, so the apparent foundation ordering is explicitly disclaimed. The 0.77 plateau shared by compositional moral and syntax is flagged as possibly a probe ceiling rather than a model ceiling, and the disambiguation experiment (repeating at 7B and 32B) is *not run*. The compositional probe is not a moral-reasoning probe; counterfactual sensitivity, novel structures and adversarial consistency are out of scope. Every finding is one model family, English only. Three standard validity controls (leave-lexeme-out, paraphrase transfer, adversarial lexical swap) are described in an appendix and then **deliberately not run** - the author argues the compositional leave-construction-out ablation is a strictly stronger test for the relevant question and leaves paraphrase transfer to future work. The author also states the estimator's two structural limits up front: critical noise is floor-dominated when baseline accuracy is already below threshold (so part of the early rise tracks accuracy crossing 0.6, not noise tolerance), and it is right-censored at the grid maximum. RMS normalization equalizes scale but not covariance shape, so a residual gradient near 2× could be anisotropy or effective dimensionality rather than robustness, and a whitened or participation-ratio-matched control is named as the experiment that would settle it and not run. The one cell not re-run under RMS is the LoRA experiment itself. The fragility gap of 0.44 in SNR units clears zero only marginally.

## What we take from it
**A measure.** Critical-noise-under-probe-perturbation as a second readout alongside accuracy, for exactly the regime we will spend most of our time in: once a linear probe saturates, our white-box model's decodability numbers stop carrying information, and a margin/redundancy-flavoured number keeps resolving. It is cheap (one trained probe, one noise sweep) and it needs no new data.

**A framing to inherit, in the author's own scoping, which is smarter than the headline.** The paper ends where the folder has repeatedly ended: a metric that moves is not a metric that means something until the scale it moves with is matched. The v2 correction - same data, same probe, same figure, conclusion withdrawn once activation scale is normalized - is the cleanest worked example in this folder of a *within-instrument* confound, as distinct from the cross-instrument disagreements the folder has been filing.

**Two warnings.**
- *Onset ordering as a lexical-floor readout, not a capability readout.* The measured coupling here is direct: the dataset with the highest bag-of-words floor (0.86) onsets first, the one with the lowest (0.59) onsets last, and the author prints that correlation rather than the tidy developmental story it would otherwise support.
- *A 16-example, single-seed cell per condition.* Appendix A is published anyway, with its own unreliability stated in the same paragraph as its numbers - the right shape, and a reminder that our own per-condition cells need their item counts printed beside them.

**A shortcut worth copying.** The compositional dataset's four construction families plus leave-construction-out transfer is a cheap, principled way to make a probe prove it generalizes beyond the specific tokens it was fitted on, measured against a pair-disjoint unigram floor. That is the same control as the folder's repeated "beat the bag-of-words baseline under a leak-free split", and this dataset shows how to build the construct so the test has teeth (hold the morally-loaded verb fixed; vary only mild tokens).

**Not to take.** Nothing here is predictive of behaviour under intervention; the causal appendix is explicitly uncommitted. If we adopt fragility, it goes in the diagnostic column, not the prediction column.

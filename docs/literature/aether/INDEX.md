Findings are about how heads escape narrow descriptions: early-layer induction heads copy literally while later ones match on "fuzzy" features (-ea sound ↔ newline after "ee" sound) with an identical OV copy circuit; a "line width" head also glues the halves of tokenised years (1 + 776) and long words, three behaviours that separate cleanly in PCA, yet a year sweep shows it only fires for 1000–1986, a constraint neither attention patterns nor per-example attributions revealed; "answer selection" is really a general relevance-marking behaviour whose OV always copies and whose complexity is all in QK; and a place head suppresses attention between a country and its own cities (country token attends to the foreign city 98% of the time on 185 generated sentences; mean-subtracted country-query/city-key cosine −0.47 own vs +0.01 other, a sign flip of the +0.17 in the residual stream), which on real data is only legible once one sees an earlier head K-composing the feature there. Carried into our work: the task-agnostic held-out example stream as a required arm of any component-description evaluation, PCA as hypothesis generator only, and the token-vs-feature input trap for predictors (K-composition).

# From Recovery to Drop-off: How Action Post-training Reduces a VLM's Late-Layer Depth Decodability

- **Key:** arxiv-2608.08904
- **Authors / organisation:** Alexander Hackett (New York University; Reflex), Arnaud Denis-Remillard (Reflex; Université de Montréal), Axel Cassou (Maastricht University)
- **Date:** 2026-08-09 (v2 2026-08-14)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08904
- **Kind:** paper (ECCV 2026 Embodied Multimodal Reasoning workshop, archival proceedings track)
- **Read from:** full text
- **Relevance to us:** 2 (useful)

## What they wanted, and why
Vision-language-action models are built by taking a pretrained vision-language model and post-training it into a policy, on the hope that the backbone's visual understanding transfers. Recent work reports that it does not fully transfer, but those reports probe semantic targets such as recognition and task classification, and they are written to motivate a corrective training objective rather than to explain the trained network. The authors ask a narrower and more mechanistic question for one geometric primitive: how much depth perception stays readable from the residual stream, at which layers the readable part is lost, and which trained submodule is responsible. The larger goal is to turn "representations degrade" into a located, testable interference claim.

## What they studied
One weight-matched open pair: Molmo2-ER as the base VLM and MolmoAct2-LIBERO as its action-post-trained counterpart, sharing a 36-layer backbone of width 2560 with SigLIP2 visual tokens and identical at initialization up to the action expert. Unit of analysis: decoder layer (all 36), then submodule write (attention write or MLP write per layer), then the accumulated sum of MLP writes — the "MLP deposits" — probed separately from the residual stream they feed. Input: LIBERO frames, primary and wrist views, resized to 256 pixels, sampled at stride 5 per rollout.

## How they did it
A capacity-matched Dense Prediction Transformer head (41.8M trainable parameters, width 512), supervised by Depth-Anything-3 mono as an image-computable teacher, is trained once per model-layer cell on visual-token positions; depth accuracy is the fraction of pixels within a 1.25 ratio bound after per-image least-squares scale-shift alignment, and the split holds out 25% of rollouts so near-duplicate frames never straddle train and validation. A ridge probe (reported as R², hence SNR = R²/(1−R²)) is run as a second, higher-bias instrument, and its ordering is only used within a model because cross-model linear decodability mixes how much depth is present with how linearly it is formatted. For the causal step, each layer's contribution is treated as a deletable additive write; twelve non-overlapping three-layer windows tile the stack, a module's write is zeroed for a window during a forward pass, and a freshly trained probe reads depth at the fixed final layer 35. That is a balanced 2 × 2 × 12 design (MLP or attention, VLA or base VLM, window) against a shared clean baseline reproduced in the same forward pass. Finally, the stream and the accumulated MLP deposits are hooked at matched row order and probed with the same recipe, and per-token magnitudes of the single write and of the stream are compared on a log axis.

## What counts as an explanation here
A causal, module-level account rather than a feature dictionary or a circuit: the explanation is that the residual stream is a sum of module writes, that depth is carried most accessibly by the accumulated MLP writes, that action post-training collapses those late writes, and that the late writes actively interfere with what the final layer can be read for — demonstrated by deleting them and watching readability return. It is partial by construction: it explains final-layer decodability of one primitive, and the authors say explicitly that it is a recovery of decodability in a freshly retrained probe, not the identity of the underlying code, and that it says nothing about closed-loop policy quality.

## How they judged success
The primary metric is probe depth accuracy per layer, with RMSE reported as a robustness check in the supplement. The layerwise curve is used as its own positive control: the base model must reproduce a known drop-off-to-recovery template before action tuning. The causal test is a full comparison — for a localised effect to count, the increase must emerge from the whole 2 × 2 × 12 sweep rather than from a single privileged cell — and every ablated condition gets a freshly trained probe so that a lifting of readability cannot be an artefact of one probe's fit. Corroboration comes from the ridge probe's within-model ordering and from the observation that an identical head varies sharply across layers and, on identically ordered frames, across models; the argument is stated as ruling out a probe confound strongly rather than proving it absent.

## What they found
The base model follows the known template — depth readable at layer 0 (about 0.71), a trough near layer 8 (about 0.66), recovering to its maximum at the end (about 0.76) — and the VLA sits below it at all 36 layers, a persistent "floor" that is narrowest mid-stack (about 0.04 apart) but never closes. The degradation is not uniform: over the last eight layers the base model rises by 0.040 while the VLA falls by 0.123, reaching its global minimum of 0.506 at layer 35 against 0.752 for the base — a late "cliff" that inverts the base model's ordering. Band means sharpen the shape: early 0.705 versus 0.617, mid 0.701 versus 0.606, late 0.744 versus 0.578, final layer 0.752 versus 0.506, with a terminal slope of +0.040 against −0.123. Ablating the final MLP window (layers 33–35) raises the VLA's final-layer readability from 0.506 to 0.584, recovering 0.078 of the 0.246 gap; the preceding window gives 0.050, every earlier MLP window at most 0.036. Attention ablation in the VLA gives 0.031 in its best window, and the same late-MLP intervention in the base model gives 0.015 — within the sweep's own variation. The decomposition explains why: in the base model the accumulated MLP deposits out-decode the stream at every layer (about 0.80 peaking at 0.84 from deposits against about 0.68 to 0.75 from the stream), while in the VLA the deposits collapse over the final blocks to 0.38, below its own stream, and the ridge SNR for those late deposits falls from a majority of the depth variance to about 0.03. Magnitudes supply the mechanism for why a write can matter late: the stream's per-token magnitude is much larger than any single MLP write through the first two-thirds of the stack and only slightly over an order of magnitude larger in the final layers, so a single late write is a small residual that can nonetheless reshape a comparatively compressed vector.

## Limits and open problems they name
One model pair, one dataset, one probe class (a DPT head) corroborated by one ridge probe; "ground truth" is a strong monocular estimator, so the curves measure agreement with that estimator rather than metric depth; depth is necessary but not sufficient for spatiogeometric understanding; the treatment is the whole bundled action post-training pipeline rather than any one stage; the ablation probes are single-seed and the evidence rests on the structured dissociation rather than on the significance of one cell; and nothing here shows that removing the writes improves a policy. They also state that the deposit-versus-stream gap's origin is an open question, offering a hypothesis about large early token magnitudes drowning out the depth signal.

## What we take from it
A method, a control and a framing. The method is the **write-level deletion sweep as a causal locator**: if a module's contribution is an additive term, zeroing that term and re-reading the site with a freshly trained probe turns "salience" into "interference", and a recovery under subtraction is a much stronger claim than a correlation. The control is the two-axis matched sweep — same module type and same window in a weight-matched sibling model that did not receive the treatment — which is what makes the 0.078 count while 0.015 does not; a targeted ablation without its sibling-model counterpart would have been uninterpretable. The framing worth adopting for our own reports is the separation of a **persistent uniform deficit** (here the floor, present at every layer and narrowest mid-stack) from a **late, compounding one** (the cliff, 0.246 at the final layer), because the two have different repair paths and a single headline "degrades representation" number destroys exactly the distinction that matters. A warning to carry as well: the ablation restores readability, not the code, and the authors say so in the sentence that reports the number.

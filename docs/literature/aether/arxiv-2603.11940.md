# Exhaustive Circuit Mapping of a Single-Cell Foundation Model Reveals Massive Redundancy, Heavy-Tailed Hub Architecture, and Layer-Dependent Differentiation Control

- **Key:** arxiv-2603.11940
- **Authors / organisation:** Ihor Kendiukhov; Department of Computer Science, University of Tübingen, Germany (self-funded)
- **Date:** 2026-03-12
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11940
- **Kind:** paper
- **Read from:** full text
- **Relevance to us:** 2 (useful)

## What they wanted, and why
Mechanistic work on biological foundation models had been built on three shortcuts: tracing a hand-picked handful of features, ablating only pairs, and reading differentiation behaviour off observational trajectories. The author argues each shortcut imports a bias - the first because the features chosen were the ones already annotated with known biology, the second because pair-only tests cannot see whether a third feature adds a new logical gate, the third because co-varying with a process is not the same as driving it. The paper replaces all three inside one model: trace every active feature at one layer, ablate in threes, and steer activations to see which way the cell state moves. The larger goal is a map of a biological foundation model that is not bounded by what humans have already named.

## What they studied
Geneformer V2-316M, an 18-layer, 18-head transformer pretrained on roughly 100 million single-cell transcriptomes, with TopK sparse autoencoders (k = 32, four-times overcomplete: a 1,152-wide residual stream lifted to 4,608 SAE units) trained per layer on residual-stream activations. The unit of analysis is the SAE feature and, for the combinatorial experiment, the triplet of features; readings are taken at layers 6, 11 and 17 when layer 5 is the source, and steering is applied at layers 0, 5, 11 and 17. Data are K562 CRISPRi perturbation cells for tracing and ablation, and 481 Tabula Sapiens immune cells with diffusion pseudotime for steering.

## How they did it
Tracing is causal ablation, not attention inspection: for each of the 4,065 layer-5 features active at frequency at least 0.001, the feature's TopK coefficient is set to zero, a forward pass is run from that layer, the downstream hidden states are encoded through the downstream SAEs, and the difference between clean and ablated activations is summarised as a Cohen's d computed with an online accumulator. An edge survives if the effect size clears 0.5 in absolute value and consistency clears 0.7. The expensive part was made cheap by caching the clean pass once for all cells, which the author reports cut the run from an estimated twenty days to 17.7 hours on a single laptop GPU. The ablation experiment takes eight triplets drawn from four pathways (vesicle transport, mitosis, metabolism, and one cross-pathway DNA-damage-response times mitosis combination), runs all seven nonempty subsets of each plus a clean baseline on 200 K562 cells, and computes a redundancy ratio as the absolute triplet effect divided by the sum of the three single effects, with the higher-order interaction term by inclusion-exclusion. Steering multiplies one SAE coefficient by 2 or 5, reconstructs the residual stream along the decoder direction, propagates to the logits, and scores the change as the shift in cosine similarity toward a late-pseudotime gene signature minus the same quantity toward an early-pseudotime signature.

## What counts as an explanation here
A circuit graph: a directed, weighted adjacency of feature-to-feature causal edges with significance thresholds, plus per-node connective degree, plus a signed steering effect per feature. It is explicitly partial in the complete-model sense - one source layer, three downstream targets - and the author calls the 1,393,850 edges a lower bound. It is predictive in a narrow sense: given a feature and a downstream target, the map predicts whether zeroing the source moves the target and roughly by how much.

## How they judged success
Cohen's d effect size with a consistency threshold, Welford's online algorithm for streaming statistics, the redundancy ratio and inclusion-exclusion interaction term, a superadditive/ additive/ subadditive partition of target instances, fraction-of-steered-cells-with-positive-shift, absolute state-shift magnitude, and an annotation-enrichment comparison of hub features against the overall annotation rate. The controls are the companion study's selective tracing (30 annotated features, 200 cells, 12 downstream layers) and the clean baseline in every ablation condition. There is no held-out circuit, no second model, and no random-feature circuit as a null.

## What they found
Exhaustive tracing yields 1,393,850 significant edges against 52,116 from the 30-feature selective pass, which the abstract calls a 27-fold expansion, with a mean of 342.9 and median 284 edges per feature and a maximum of 2,138; eight features have no significant edges at all. The degree distribution is heavy-tailed: 72 features (1.8%) exceed a thousand edges and 759 (18.7%) exceed five hundred. Effects attenuate with distance from the source layer - 694,646 edges at L6 (49.8%), 443,381 at L11 (31.8%), 255,823 at L17 (18.4%), a 2.7-fold fall the author reads as dilution. Eight of the top twenty hubs (40%) carry no GO, KEGG or Reactome annotation against a 53.8% baseline annotation rate over all features, and the paper reports no significant enrichment of annotation among the top hundred or top twenty. The largest hub is F898 with 2,138 edges, followed by F1762 with 2,098. On ablation, the three-way redundancy ratio is 0.59 for same-pathway triplets and 0.56 for the cross-pathway one, both below the pairwise 0.74, with the marginal third-feature effect given the first two ablated running 0.006 to 0.183 by median; over 5,000 target instances with at least one significant effect, 4,703 (94.1%) are subadditive, 290 (5.8%) additive and 7 (0.14%) superadditive. On steering, all three L17 features move cells toward maturity in 100% of steered cells, four L11 features sit at 0.00 to 0.44 with a mean of 0.26, five L0 features span 0.08 to 0.58 and two L5 features span 0.43 to 0.91, while the absolute magnitudes are around 0.001 to 0.003 at alpha 5 among roughly 4,600 SAE units. The paper notes the layer trend is not strictly monotonic, since the L11 mean sits below the L0 mean. (The marginal range is printed as 0.006 to 0.183 in the prose and as 0.184 in Table 3's largest median cell; both are quoted where they appear.)

## Limits and open problems they name
Twenty cells per feature instead of two hundred cuts statistical power and likely explains the lower per-feature edge count relative to selective tracing, and three downstream layers instead of twelve means intermediate effects are unmeasured. Three-way ablation covers only eight triplets over four pathways. Steering effects are small in absolute terms, so the author flags biological significance as open even though direction is consistent. Everything is one model; replication in scGPT, scBERT or protein foundation models is left undone. The graph is also only traced from layer 5, so the claim that redundancy is an architectural principle rests on one source layer.

## What we take from it
Three concrete borrowings. (1) A measure: **annotation enrichment is not a relevance proxy, and the paper prices it** - a feature's known label tells you almost nothing about its causal fan-out, so any selection step that filters on human-named units silently truncates the graph, which is the same failure our read-site choices can make when they are restricted to components a dictionary already names. (2) A form: **subadditivity reported as a ratio against the additive sum, with an explicit superadditive count**, is the cheapest way to say that single-component effects do not compose, and the marginal third-term statistic (effect of adding one more member given the rest ablated) is a direct measure of how much a growing explanation set is still buying. (3) A warning: an exhaustive-looking map is still conditional on one source layer and three targets, and the paper's own numbers show the cost - the per-feature edge count drops by a factor of about five when cells per feature drop from two hundred to twenty, which is a reminder that a circuit map's density is a function of its statistical budget rather than of the network alone. Also reusable: caching the clean pass once and running ablations from the cached state is the compute trick that makes exhaustive intervention affordable at all.

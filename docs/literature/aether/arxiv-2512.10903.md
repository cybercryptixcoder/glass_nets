# Multi-Granular Node Pruning for Causal Circuit Discovery

- **Key:** arxiv-2512.10903
- **Authors / organisation:** Muhammad Umair Haider, Hammad Rizwan, Hassan Sajjad, A.B. Siddique; University of Kentucky and Dalhousie University
- **Date:** 2026-08-09 (v3; cc-by)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10903
- **Kind:** paper
- **Read from:** full text
- **Relevance to us:** 3 (central)

## What they wanted, and why

Circuit discovery methods mostly delete *edges* between coarse components (attention heads, MLP blocks) while holding task behaviour fixed, which is expensive and cannot say anything about the neurons inside a head or block. The authors want the minimal subnetwork for a behaviour to be expressible at several levels at once — whole blocks, heads, MLP blocks, individual neurons — and to be found in one optimisation rather than by iterative pruning. Their motivation is both cost (edge pruning stores every intermediate activation and scales with the square of the node count) and resolution (a head can mix unrelated functions, so a head-level circuit can be too coarse to be the mechanism). The larger goal is the folder's usual one: a compact, causally grounded description of what a network does on a task.

## What they studied

GPT-2 small (124.5M parameters, 12 layers) on three standard circuit tasks — Indirect Object Identification, Gendered Pronouns, Greater Than. Scalability runs on Llama-3.2-1B and Llama-3.1-8B on IOI and GP, and GPT-2-XL (1.5B, 48 layers, 25 heads per layer) on all three. The unit of analysis is the **node at a chosen granularity**: attention block, MLP block, attention head, attention neuron, MLP first-layer (key) neuron, MLP second-layer (value) neuron.

## How they did it

Two forward streams are run: a clean prompt and a corrupted prompt produced by a task-specific corruption that flips the correct answer (a different start year, a swapped name, an opposite-gender name). Each node's output is an interpolation between its clean and corrupted activation, controlled by a mask in [0,1]; masks are drawn from the Hard Concrete distribution so gradients flow but the relaxed mask concentrates near 0 and 1. Training minimises a weighted sum of three terms: a KL divergence between the full model's next-token distribution and the circuit's, a margin-ranking loss that requires the correct option's logit to beat the distractor's by a margin, and an expected-L0 sparsity penalty on the masks, with the sparsity penalty normalised within each granularity and then renormalised across granularities so that one coefficient controls total sparsity. After training the masks are binarised at the Hard Concrete threshold and hierarchy is enforced: if a block's mask is zero, its heads and neurons are zeroed too. Training uses 150-200 examples per task, batch 32, 500 epochs, Adam at a learning rate of 0.03, sequence length 64, on one A100-40GB. Baselines are EAP (gradient edge attribution), Edge Pruning (learned edge masks), and DiscoGP (joint edge and unstructured weight masks). Because the baselines report edges and this method reports nodes, size is compared across granularities by two deliberately conservative proxies: an edge method's node is counted pruned only if it is an island with no surviving incoming or outgoing edge (found by reverse breadth-first search), and this method's edge count assumes every surviving node is fully connected.

## What counts as an explanation here

A **selection** — a binary mask over nodes, at one or several granularities, that is meant to be a minimal sufficient subnetwork for the task. It is a set, not a description and not a graph of interactions; the authors say plainly that the masks do not recover which surviving node talks to which other node, nor the direction or multiplicity of the interaction. Its predictive content is limited to what it preserves: the circuit is supposed to reproduce the full model's output distribution and its correct-versus-distractor margin when everything outside it is replaced by the corrupted stream.

## How they judged success

Task-specific behaviour scores: an IOI score (indirect-object logit minus repeated-subject logit), a GP score (gender-consistent minus inconsistent pronoun logit), and a GT score (probability mass more than ten years after the start year minus mass more than ten before), each also binarised into accuracy. Fidelity: KL divergence between full-model and circuit output distributions, plus logit difference. Size: percentage pruned at each granularity (attention blocks, attention heads, MLP blocks, attention neurons, MLP key neurons, MLP value neurons) and a conservative edge-compression percentage. Second-order checks: a sweep of the sparsity coefficient for each model, five mask-initialisation seeds on the Llama IOI circuit, post-hoc edge pruning on top of the node circuits, and a qualitative head-role analysis by direct logit attribution.

## What they found

On GPT-2, node pruning reaches a *block-level* MLP sparsity no baseline touches: 50% of MLP blocks on IOI, 75% on GP, 50% on GT, while EAP and Edge Pruning retain every MLP block and DiscoGP reaches 16.66% on GT only. Neuron-level MLP sparsity is 73.6 / 91.1 / 90.9 percent on key neurons and 59.9 / 86.3 / 83.7 percent on value neurons, against effectively zero for all baselines. The headline "least favourable setting" figure is 33.34% more MLP blocks and 59.8% more neurons pruned than the best baseline. Fidelity is not sacrificed for this — KL is 0.466 / 0.337 / 0.048 (baselines: 0.169 / 0.070 / 0.044 for Edge Pruning, 6.687 / 5.090 / 2.812 for DiscoGP) with accuracy 0.958 / 0.984 / 0.992. Edge compression is the one axis where they lose: 93.73 / 94.69 / 99.21 percent against Edge Pruning's 94.77 / 98.88 / 98.75, and their diagnostic accepts this, showing post-hoc edge pruning recovers about 3.7 and 3.6 further percentage points on IOI and GP. Cost: 6,270 MB peak memory and 350 s wall clock, against 72,794 MB / 21 s for EAP, 33,354 MB / 2,756 s for Edge Pruning and 17,633 MB / 2,926 s for DiscoGP; only 55,465 extra mask parameters on a 124.5M-parameter model. The Llama runs prune more, not less, with scale: on GP, Llama-3.1-8B prunes 75% of blocks and 95.7% of heads at accuracy 0.99 and KL 0.23. Gold-standard fidelity on GPT-2-XL stays high even at extreme sparsity — GT keeps 0.9501 accuracy at KL 0.0047 with attention surviving in exactly four layers (23, 25, 26, 28), each retaining all 25 heads.

The structural result is the one worth carrying: the three tasks come out with qualitatively different topologies rather than different amounts of sparsity. IOI is MLP-dominant — attention pruned in many layers, MLPs active almost throughout depth, the same picture on Llama-3.1-8B. GP is localised and discontinuous — GPT-2 small keeps MLPs only in layers 0, 3 and 7, Llama-3.2-1B concentrates its MLPs in the first three layers. GT is skip-like — GPT-2 small has a contiguous dead block in layers 2-5 with one large MLP in layer 0 and computation resuming at layers 8-11, and GPT-2-XL concentrates all attention into four layers. The Llama-3.2-1B IOI circuit keeps 58 of 512 heads, concentrated in layers 8-11 and 14-15, and direct logit attribution recovers the by-now-standard roles in them (IO-movers, negative IO-movers, subject inhibitors, weak subject promoters) — the same functional decomposition Wang et al. found by hand in GPT-2.

## Limits and open problems they name

The one limitation the authors state outright is the interaction gap already described: node masks say *which* units are necessary, not *how* they communicate, and they propose a hybrid node-then-edge procedure as the fix. Underneath that sit several things the results themselves show. The protocol is adversarial interpolation, which is not mean or zero ablation, so the circuits are sets of units that separate correct from corrupted behaviour rather than sets that carry the behaviour alone, and the authors explicitly reject zero/mean ablation because it invites a compensatory re-solve. Comparisons across granularities rest on proxies the authors call strict — an edge method's "node" is only an island, and their own edge count assumes full connectivity — so the block-level MLP win is partly a statement about what edge masks cannot express. The fidelity numbers are not uniformly better than Edge Pruning's: KL is higher on IOI and GP, and only GT is genuinely close. Five seeds were checked on one model and one task. GPT-2-XL's IOI circuit holds KL 0.5568 at 97.52% accuracy, which is a real fidelity cost at scale. Safety risk is acknowledged: removing nodes can disable refusal or moderation pathways.

## What we take from it

Three things, all mechanical. **First, the granularity of the unit is a choice we make and must report as a choice:** this paper shows the same model and task yielding a block-level, a head-level and a neuron-level circuit that are all faithful, and the smallest one is not the most informative — GT on GPT-2-XL keeps all 25 heads in four layers, which is a statement about *where*, and says nothing about what those heads compute. For our white-box model we name the unit level per claim and never let a neuron-level count stand in for an explanation. **Second, the hierarchy constraint is the cheap correctness check we can borrow:** if a parent block is pruned, its children must be too, and the authors enforce it at extraction time; a mask set that violates it is internally inconsistent, which is a one-line test on any decomposition we produce. **Third, and most useful, the comparison proxies are the honest kind of lose:** they win on node sparsity, lose on edge compression (93.73 vs 94.77 on IOI), and print the loss next to the win — with the failure diagnosed rather than hidden, and repaired by a stated extra step whose memory cost they also print. That is the shape we want for any of our own coverage numbers. A warning goes with it: the paper's "many neurons deemed important by coarse methods are irrelevant" claim is established only for the adversarial-interpolation fidelity definition, and run 752-753's dose result is the standing reason not to read a node's small causal effect under one intervention as evidence it does nothing.

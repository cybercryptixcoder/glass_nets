## What they wanted, and why

Every probe in this folder publishes a read-out and then has to answer an awkward follow-up: is the model's own behaviour informed by that read-out, or is the probe reading something the model has no access to? The authors import a neuroscience instrument to answer it directly. A neurofeedback loop takes a scalar measurement of the subject's internal state, shows the subject that scalar, and asks them to report it or to change it. Applied to a language model, the measurement is a projection of the model's own residual stream onto a chosen axis, the "display" is an in-context label, and the requested action is either classification of the next sentence's label or steering of the model's own activations toward a target label. The motivation is double-edged and stated as such: if models can monitor and control their activations, in-context examples become a route for evading latent-space monitors — a white-box attack that needs no gradient. The ambition behind the specific experiment is a vocabulary for saying that a model represents something and a model can tell you about it are separate facts. The framing is deliberately operational rather than philosophical — the paper says outright that it is not trying to settle what metacognition is.

## What they studied

Seven instruction-tuned models across two families: LLaMA-3.2-1B, LLaMA-3.2-3B, LLaMA-3.1-8B and LLaMA-3.1-70B (Meta Llama 3 Community License), and Qwen2.5-1B, Qwen2.5-3B, Qwen2.5-7B (Apache 2.0). Four ETHICS-style corpora: the commonsense-morality subset of ETHICS (main), a true/false statement set, a happy/sad emotion set, and a sycophancy set synthesised by prompting Claude Opus 4.1 along two axes (sycophantic/sincere, agreeing/disagreeing). Each dataset gives 1,200 sentences split in half — 600 to fit the directions, 600 for the neurofeedback dialogues — with labels balanced at the median. The unit of analysis is a **direction in the residual stream at one layer**, either a logistic-regression weight vector trained to predict the dataset's semantic label (taken as the "semantically interpretable" axis) or a principal component of that layer's activations (taken as the "variance-explaining" axis), with components at ranks 1, 2, 4, 8, 32, 128 and 512 sampled to span variance from large to negligible. Five layers per model, at 0/25/50/75/100% of depth. No weights are trained and nothing is fine-tuned; the only learning in the model is in-context.

## How they did it

For each sentence the residual-stream vectors are averaged over all token positions to give a sentence embedding, projected onto the chosen axis, and thresholded at the dataset median into a binary label — this is called the neurofeedback label, and it is the only definition of the target that the dialogue sees. A dialogue then presents a system prompt, then N sentence/label pairs as alternating neutral user messages and assistant messages, then a probe turn. In the **reporting** task the probe turn is a fresh sentence and the model must emit its label. In the **explicit control** task the probe turn names a label and instructs the model to imitate the manner of the examples that carry it, and the model generates a new sentence autoregressively; its activations are then measured on what it wrote. In the **implicit control** task the probe turn supplies a raw dataset sentence that the model did not generate, so any change in that sentence's activations cannot be explained by the model's own output tokens. Reporting accuracy is the sign of the logit difference between the "1" and "0" tokens at a fixed read-out position, read from logits rather than sampled. Control strength is Cohen's d between the target-axis projections gathered under instructions to imitate label 1 and to imitate label 0, with a closed-form standard error and 95% intervals. N ranges over 0, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256 examples; each configuration is run 100 times with resampled sentences. A four-cell counterbalancing scheme splits the effects of the label-to-group assignment from the effect of the instruction, so a model that merely complies with "imitate 1" cannot pass. Off-target effects are measured by projecting the same activations onto all other axes and computing a **control precision** — target effect over the mean off-target effect — with a threshold of 1 standing for "no better than spreading the change evenly". Layer-wise accumulation is tracked by an explicit alignment test: for a prompt targeting the axis defined at layer l, project every layer's residual stream on that same global direction and watch the trajectory cross zero. An ideal-observer bound (logistic regression with full access to the activations, asked to predict the same neurofeedback label) is computed as the ceiling for the reporting task, and eight-level quantised labels are run as an extension of the binary ones.

## What counts as an explanation here

A **capability claim about a direction**, quantified: not "this direction encodes morality" (the probe already says that) but "a model given N examples and a scalar read-out per sentence can re-present that scalar as an output token", scored against an upper bound, plus "a model told to move that scalar can move it, by this effect size, at this depth, and mostly without moving other things". The formal apparatus is a two-level definition borrowed from circuit description — first-order nodes are the task circuit, second-order nodes are downstream processes that report or control it, and a second-order node must not be needed for baseline task performance. The explanation is explicitly partial: an axis and a depth, plus a geometric description of how precisely control isolates the target, plus a decomposition of where along the stack the control accumulates. It is meant to predict under intervention in the sense that a direction which survives the metacognitive test is one whose value the model can condition its surprising effects on; a direction that fails it is one whose causal relevance, if any, is outside the model's reach.

## How they judged success

Reporting: accuracy and cross-entropy as a function of N, against an ideal-observer linear classifier as an upper bound and against repeated-axis baselines (each principal component) to convert the absolute number into a ranking. Control: Cohen's d on the target axis, with 95% intervals from the closed-form standard error; layer, N and target axis swept orthogonally; 100 repeats per cell; explicit versus implicit as the two ends of a difficulty spectrum; target-versus-off-target heat maps with control precision as the selectivity measure. Cross-dataset replication on four corpora, cross-model replication on seven models, and the 70B model run only at N = 256 because of cost. An extremity test: whether the instructed state exceeds the range observed under no instruction at all. Accumulation: per-layer projections onto the prompt-targeted global direction, with the layer that defines the axis marked.

## What they found

**Reporting**: accuracy rises with the number of examples and is highest on the logistic-regression axis — the axis whose direction is defined by a semantic contrast — and it plateaus early there, while the principal components improve more slowly and earlier components are reported better than late ones. So the two properties the authors hypothesised would matter both do: semantic interpretability of the axis and how much variance it explains. Reporting accuracy varies by layer in a way neither of those two properties accounts for, which the paper names as an unexplained residual. Everything sits well below the ideal observer, and that gap is the load-bearing result: the information is in the activations in principle, and the model reports only part of it.

**Control**: with enough examples the model moves its own neural scores in the instructed direction, on the logistic-regression axis most strongly, and effect sizes increase with depth and with model size across both families — LLaMA-3.1-8B and LLaMA-3.1-70B in the main figure, Qwen2.5-7B in an appendix panel — with LLaMA consistently stronger than Qwen at matched relative depth and similar size. The 70B model can be driven past the range its activations occupy when no one asks it to control anything; the prompt never requests an extreme, so the authors read this as a floor on what a better-built prompt could achieve. Implicit control — same instruction, no generated tokens to scaffold on — produces the same qualitative ordering at much smaller magnitude, and in early layers it is close to zero.

**Selectivity**: off-target effects are extensive and organised. Prompts aimed at early components are more precise than prompts aimed at late ones, and the precision ratio crosses 1 somewhere between components 32 and 128 — the paper therefore places the controllable subspace between 32 and 128 dimensions, on both 8B and 70B. The column-wise reading is the more interesting one: to move an early component, the prompt aimed at that component is the best way, but to move a late component other prompts do a better job than the one aimed at it, i.e. the control levers are not recoverable by inversion at the low-variance end. Precision is uniformly worse under implicit control.

**Accumulation**: effects on a direction defined at layer l build gradually before l and can plateau or keep growing past it, distributed over several layers rather than appearing at one, for both explicit and implicit control, with different shapes on different corpora and models. An axis defined by averaging per-layer classifiers behaves about like the axis from a single middle layer.

**Safety reading**, stated as an implication rather than shown in deployment: because labels come from the model's own states and the instruction is a plain-language imitation request, this is an in-context, gradient-free route to making activations look benign — implicit control being the covert version, since the sentence was not generated by the model. Two defensive suggestions follow from the paper's own structure: place monitors in subspaces that the model cannot control (the late components), and monitor several independent subspaces at once in the hope that coordinating contradictory controls exceeds the model's capacity. The authors also position the paradigm against patching, whose interventions can push activations off the natural data manifold, whereas a model that is instructed rather than edited stays inside the distribution it normally visits.

## Limits and open problems they name

A single axis, a single layer, a single attempt per sentence — no multi-layer or multi-axis control except a preliminary averaged-classifier appendix; residual stream only, not attention heads, MLP internals or layer-wise logits; only two axis families, with sparse-autoencoder features and transcoder circuits named as untried; a small slice of the reporting/control landscape (no confidence judgments, error monitoring or wagering). "Metacognitive space" is estimated from a proxy, not measured: the 32–128 boundary is where the control-precision ratio crosses 1 on two models, not a boundary of a defined capacity. The instruction-following requirement is itself a confound for the control task — a model that ignores the imitation instruction and simply produces whatever its context favours would look like failed control, and the authors address this by conditioning on the counterbalanced design rather than by separately measuring instruction compliance. Ethical framing of the anthropomorphic language is explicitly disclaimed in a footnote. The paper's own framing of the strongest control case — 70B going past the natural range — rests on headline prose and a single panel rather than a table of per-cell numbers.

## What we take from it

A **screening test for our own probe directions**, and the clearest one in the folder: before claiming a direction is a cause, ask whether the model can report it and whether the model can move it, with both scored against a linear-decoder ceiling and against off-target axes. A direction that is reportable and controllable is one whose value the network's second-order machinery can bind to a token; a direction that is decodable and neither reportable nor controllable is a representational fact with no visible handle — exactly the case the folder keeps meeting from the other side (`arxiv-2610.21708`: a decodable belief that is inert when patched; `arxiv-2605.14038`: cognition and action probes collapsing to orthogonality at the read-out). What is new here is that the test requires **no intervention and no surrogate**: only logits from a dialogue, which makes it the cheapest complement to the patching battery and the natural next experiment for any direction we publish.

Three design rules travel with it and are cheap to adopt. (1) **Always report a decoder ceiling beside an in-context score.** The ideal observer is one logistic regression on the same activations; without it, "the model reports this axis at above chance" is unquantified, because the ceiling differs wildly across axes. (2) **Score selectivity, not only effect.** Control precision is an off-target ratio, which is the same instrument `arxiv-2606.25092` had to invent for expert ablations, arrived at independently on the direction side — and its crossing point is the interesting number, because it converts "the model can move X" into "the model can move X without moving everything else". (3) **Separate explicit from implicit control**, because the difference between an effect that rides on the model's own output tokens and one that appears on foreign input is exactly the difference between an effect that a black-box filter could catch and one it could not.

Two warnings to carry. A **variance-explained axis is not a semantic axis and the two behave differently**, so a "best direction" found by PCA in our own work should not be compared to a probe direction on the same scale — the folder's direction work is almost all probe-type, and this paper prices what is lost by using the other kind. And **the whole report is behavioural in-context evidence about a directional scalar**: nothing here shows the metacognitive machinery a monitor can rely on, it shows that in-context pressure is sufficient to move one, which is the adversarial reading the authors themselves foreground.

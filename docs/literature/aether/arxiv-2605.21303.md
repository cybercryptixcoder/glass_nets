## What they wanted, and why

Mechanistic interpretability keeps producing circuits, and the outputs do not accumulate: each study uses its own dataset, model and protocol and summarises the result in an informal label. The authors' complaint is not that discovery is weak but that there is no representation of a finding that another experiment could compare against, refine, or reuse. They separate three missing pieces - a language for saying what a circuit computes, criteria for deciding whether two claims agree or contradict, and principles for reusing a claim on a new model - and call the gap formal coherence. The reframing they propose is to treat circuit interpretation as inductive theory construction, so that a discovered subgraph becomes a typed logical object rather than a figure in a paper. The larger aim is cumulative mechanistic science: a registry of records that can be queried, ordered, and transferred, which is close to what a white-box model of a network would have to be if it is to be reused across checkpoints.

## What they studied

Circuits over attention heads and MLP blocks in three models: Pythia-14M, Pythia-1B, and LLaMA-3.2-1B. Tasks are grouped into three operation families - semantic role binding (Location, Time, Path, plus Goal, Beneficiary, Source, Instrument, Topic in the expanded pool), indirect object identification as a *selection* task, and Greater-Than as a *comparison* task. The primary evaluation is 15 circuits (5 tasks x 3 models); the scaled evaluation is 30 circuits per model from 10 tasks discovered on three disjoint prompt subsets. Circuits are extracted with EAP-IG retaining 200 edges and 3 integrated-gradient steps, so the unit of analysis is an attributed subgraph with node types and edge attributions, not a direction or a feature.

## How they did it

Every circuit is first stored as four layers of Prolog-style ground facts: provenance (task, model, discovery method), structure (nodes, types, layers, edges, component ratio, relative size, layer span, named structural motifs such as a three-hop attention chain or an MLP-heavy composition, and a normalised faithfulness ratio), a causal-functional layer, and the learned signature. The causal-functional signature is built per node: direct logit attribution gives each node an additive score for the correct answer, nodes below a small threshold are flagged causally marginal, and the surviving nodes get an attention profile over input positions weighted by that score, with positions labelled by shared linguistic roles (subject, object, verb, function, entity, other) and by task-specific roles. For the structural half they run inductive logic programming: positive examples are circuits from the target task, negatives are circuits from other tasks, background knowledge is the fact set, and the learner searches two hypothesis spaces - one over hand-named motifs, one over blind structural predicates (edge types, layer span, composition) so the result is not limited to the authors' vocabulary. Motifs are ranked by information gain multiplied by one plus their mean edge attribution, and clauses are selected by one-vs-rest F1 with a preference for shorter clauses. A circuit is kept only if it preserves behaviour above a threshold and loses task accuracy when its edges are ablated, with approximate minimality checked by greedy edge removal. Learned clauses are then compared by theta-subsumption, a substitution-based ordering that says one clause generalises, specialises, equals, or is incomparable to another; the relaxed version drops numeric thresholds so the ordering captures predicate structure only. Transfer treats the source signature as a retrieval hypothesis: find candidates in the target registry whose scale-normalised predicates and signature distance fall inside a tolerance, screen them behaviourally and causally on target data, take the best, and fall back to full rediscovery if none passes. Two transfer directions are run into Pythia-1B - from Pythia-14M (cross-scale, a 70x parameter gap) and from LLaMA-3.2-1B (cross-family) - each with a 30-circuit target registry.

## What counts as an explanation here

A logical record: a four-tuple of causal-functional signature, learned Horn clause, clause-complexity summary, and validation statistics. The clause is meant to be the explanatory object - a readable, falsifiable structural claim of the form "this circuit is of type task if it has these motifs, this layer span and this composition" - while the signature says what the circuit computes in terms of which token roles its components attend to. It is deliberately partial and structural: the authors say plainly that a clause is not tested for predicting task behaviour on novel prompts, only for predicting causal contribution across models, and that the taxonomy of operation types is a schema for three task families rather than a theory of internal mechanisms. Prediction is present but narrow - the signature predicts which candidate in another model is the same kind of mechanism, which is a retrieval prediction rather than a behavioural one.

## How they judged success

Three measures, one per research question. For representation: whether the causal-functional profiles cluster by task family (a permutation test on Pythia-1B feature vectors gave p = 0.008) and whether the dominant linguistic role is stable across model scale. For comparison: ILP signature distance on five circuits, compared with a Weisfeiler-Lehman graph kernel, a random forest trained on the same structural features (graded by leave-one-out accuracy), and Euclidean distance over the same vectors; reported as an "IOI separation" ratio, plus a sensitivity curve over the acceptance threshold. For transfer: accepted candidates over total candidates, whether the accepted best match is task-correct, source and target causal contribution, and a random-subgraph control - 50 size-matched random subgraphs per circuit, one-sided permutation tests at alpha = 0.05. Subsumption is judged by whether the induced partial order is non-trivial.

## What they found

The two mechanisms separate cleanly at the level of what the circuits do: on Pythia-1B the IOI circuit spends 85 of its 200 edges on attention-to-attention connections and forms three-hop attention chains, while semantic role circuits spend 64 to 96 edges on MLP-to-MLP flow, and LLaMA reproduces the pattern (44 attention-attention edges for IOI against 0 to 2 for roles). Dominant attended role tracks the task rather than the scale - Location and Path are object-dominant at every scale, Time is function-dominant throughout - with one interesting shift: IOI moves from object-dominant at 14M to function-dominant at 1B and on LLaMA. Structural distance mirrors this. In the five-task pool IOI sits at d = 0.32-0.45 from everything else, roles and Greater-Than cluster at d = 0.05-0.13, and ILP achieves 3.9x IOI separation on Pythia-1B (4.2x on LLaMA) against 1.3x for the WL kernel; the random forest, given the same features, reaches 60% leave-one-out accuracy and misclassifies GT and IOI. Relaxed subsumption gives a non-trivial hierarchy: the single-predicate IOI clause generalises everything, Time generalises Path, and Location is incomparable to both - a partial order that changes with scale, since in Pythia-14M a size-only clause generalises all and Location and Path become equivalent. In the ten-task pool confidence stays high for structurally stable tasks (IOI 0.93, GT 0.89) and collapses for prompt-sensitive roles (Location and Beneficiary both 0.31), and the within-task/between-task distance ratio is 2.3x for LLaMA IOI but near 1.0 for most roles - meaning three prompt splits of a role circuit are as dissimilar from each other as from other tasks. Transfer works as retrieval: node overlap across models is essentially nil (Jaccard 0.09-0.24 within Pythia), yet all five tasks are accepted in both directions; IOI and GT find their exact task match (8/41 and 2/37-41 candidates accepted), while every semantic role selects Goal, a role absent from the source pool, on the grounds of the same binding motif vocabulary. Causal persistence tracks mechanism type rather than architecture: Location and Path contributions differ across families by 0.08 and 0.26, Time by 0.85, IOI reverses sign between Pythia scales (-0.91 to 4.37), and Greater-Than gaps by 3.97 within Pythia and 2.74 across families. Random size-matched subgraphs give near-zero contribution, and real circuits beat them in 29/34 and 28/35 cases on the two Pythia models.

## Limits and open problems they name

The clause is falsifiable in principle but tested only against causal contribution across models, never against task behaviour on fresh prompts. The evaluation is 30 circuits over ten tasks, two Pythia scales and one cross-family model, and the authors say the binding/selection/comparison schema is not a universal account. All thresholds are chosen empirically rather than fitted, with sensitivity analysis only for the transfer distance. The causal-functional profiles depend on a lightweight dependency parser for the linguistic role labels, and greedy minimality can miss non-contiguous minimal subcircuits. Extending to more distributed mechanisms and more architecture families is named as the main future direction.

## What we take from it

A framing, a measure, and a warning. **Framing:** this is the first entry in the folder that treats the *record* as the deliverable and gives it a formal comparison relation - the four-layer fact store plus a subsumption order is a concrete design for the part of our white-box model that says "this mechanism is what we already saw elsewhere", and the transfer protocol (retrieve by scale-invariant predicates, then validate causally on target data, then fall back to rediscovery) is a template for reusing a reading across checkpoints without claiming identity from structure alone. **Measure:** report an "IOI separation" style ratio against a same-features baseline, because the random forest here shows that the predicate inventory alone does not explain the discrimination and only the logical clause does - a control we can copy directly. **Warning:** the confidence collapse from 0.93 to 0.31 on prompt splits, and the near-1.0 within/between distance ratio for roles, is the same measurement artefact this folder keeps meeting, now at the level of the record itself: an explanatory object that is only stable for one task family out of three, under three prompt splits, is a description of a sample.

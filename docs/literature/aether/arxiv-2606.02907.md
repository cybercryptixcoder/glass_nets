# Linear Probes Detect Task Format, Not Reasoning Mode in Language Model Hidden States

- **Key:** arxiv-2606.02907
- **Authors / organisation:** Subramanyam Sahoo, Vinija Jain, Aman Chadha, Divya Chaudhary; Horizon Research / Meta / Apple / Northeastern University
- **Date:** 2026-06-01 (v2 2026-06-03; accepted at the 6th Workshop on Trustworthy NLP, ACL 2026)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02907
- **Kind:** paper
- **Read from:** full text
- **Relevance to us:** 3 (central)

## What they wanted, and why
The field routinely trains a linear probe on hidden states to separate "reasoning types" — deductive, inductive, abductive — and reads high accuracy as evidence that the model has built distinct internal machinery per type. These authors wanted to test the assumption underneath that practice: that the probe is reading reasoning structure rather than whatever else co-varies with the label. The worry is structural rather than model-specific, because the standard experimental design sources each reasoning type from a different benchmark, so label and dataset identity are perfectly correlated by construction. If that design silently manufactures the finding, an entire class of interpretability claims about reasoning is measuring vocabulary and answer-sheet shape. The larger goal is a methodological one: make format deconfounding and random-direction controls routine before any geometry is read as computation.

## What they studied
One model, Qwen3-14B (14B, 40 layers, hidden width 5120, bfloat16), in non-thinking inference mode with the model's internal reasoning blocks explicitly disabled. The stimulus set is 750 multiple-choice items, 250 each from LogiQA 2.0 (deductive), ARC-Challenge (inductive) and αNLI (abductive); labels come from dataset provenance, not from any post-hoc classification of the trace. The unit of analysis is the residual-stream hidden state at the last input token, swept over every layer, plus the generated reasoning traces and a per-item output confidence. Only correctly answered items enter the geometric analysis.

## How they did it
Layer by layer, a logistic probe (L2, C fixed at 1.0, stratified 5-fold CV) predicts the three-way reasoning-mode label from the last-token state, and the best layer is selected by accuracy. At that layer they also compute manifold descriptors: TwoNN intrinsic dimensionality, local SVD curvature, centroid separation ratios, and a kNN approximation of hull contamination. Then a four-stage confound pipeline. First, an identical probe predicts dataset source instead of mode — if source accuracy equals mode accuracy, the probe cannot be told apart from a source detector. Second, a logistic model using only the option count (2 vs 4) is fitted, to see how much of the separation a single surface scalar buys. Third, they restrict to the two 4-choice sets and re-probe, to isolate non-option-count format differences. Fourth, and centrally, they build a format feature vector (source one-hot, option count, response length), fit a Ridge regression from those features to the hidden state, and re-probe the residuals for both mode and source. Independently of probing they measure trace-mode agreement: each generated trace is embedded from the model's last-layer state and assigned to whichever hand-written mode anchor description ("applies a known rule step by step", etc.) it is closest to by cosine, with chance at one third. Finally they run a causal arm — mean-difference steering between mode centroids injected at the probe's best layer, a magnitude chosen by a coherence sweep with an Otsu-derived threshold, evaluated on a small pool of previously wrong items, and scored against 20 Gaussian random directions of equal norm at the same layer using a Laplace-corrected empirical p-value. A conflict-injection arm adds two opposing steering vectors at once, with 10 random-pair controls, and classifies the outcome as collapse, dominance or hybrid against baseline percentiles.

## What counts as an explanation here
A methodological decomposition rather than a model of the network. The explanatory object is a claim about *what a probing result means*: it splits a high probe score into a format-attributable share and a residual share, and asserts that once format is regressed out nothing reasoning-specific remains. It is a falsification instrument — a way to say "this measurement is a read-out of the prompt, not of the computation" — and it predicts nothing about the network's behaviour on new inputs. The positive claim the paper settles for is descriptive: a single uniform strategy, not three mode-specific circuits.

## How they judged success
Balanced cross-validated probe accuracy against a 33.3% chance level, with a 5000-permutation test on the raw result; bootstrap confidence intervals (2000 resamples) reported as the paper's general protocol. The diagnostic criterion is comparison across probes: mode accuracy vs source accuracy, and raw vs residual accuracy. Geometry is scored with named descriptors (intrinsic dimensionality, separation ratio, hull contamination) rather than eyeballed clusters. Behavioural evidence is trace-mode agreement against chance. Causality is scored by targeted-versus-random steering accuracy recovery with Cohen's d and an empirical p-value, plus a magnitude control through conflict injection. The authors are explicit that all thresholds, including the steering magnitude and the conflict cut-offs, are learned from the data distributions rather than tuned by hand.

## What they found
At layer 32 of 40 the three-way probe reaches 100% balanced accuracy with perfect per-class precision, recall and F1, and the geometry looks exactly as the literature expects: mode-specific intrinsic dimensionalities of 20.6 (deductive), 28.5 (inductive) and 33.6 (abductive), separation ratios above one for every pair, hull contamination at or below 1.5%, and three clean UMAP clusters. A source-predicting probe at the same layer also gets 100%, which is the first sign the two labels are the same object. Option count alone gets 33.3% mode accuracy — exactly the prior for the single 2-choice class — and restricting to the two 4-choice sets leaves near-perfect separation, so count is one contributor and vocabulary/style are others. After Ridge residualisation of source, option count and length, both the mode probe and the source probe fall to about 33.5%, i.e. chance. Trace-mode agreement is 42.5% against a 33.3% chance line even though the model answers 86.0% of items correctly overall (LogiQA 73.2%, ARC 93.6%, αNLI 91.2%) — it is competent everywhere and switches strategy almost never. Steering toward another mode recovers 40.0% of targeted accuracy against 31.7% for the random directions, with p = 0.286 and a small effect size, and conflict injection collapses coherence 100% of the time for both targeted and random pairs — the effect is magnitude, not direction. Layer-wise steering shows larger shifts from early-layer intervention, but those shifts too are indistinguishable from random at the same magnitude.

## Limits and open problems they name
One model only; replication across families is called necessary but not done. The residual analysis is the conservative version, since the source one-hot can absorb nearly all variance, and the authors leave the intermediate test — residualising only option count and length, keeping source — to future work, which is exactly the test that would tell us whether the separation is purely source identity or broader format. Trace-mode agreement rests on cosine similarity to hand-written anchors, which may miss fine-grained differences. The 2-vs-4 option-count asymmetry is an admitted confound. The causal arm is small: up to 15 previously wrong items, 20 random directions, and the reported p-value corresponds to 5 of 20 random directions matching the targeted recovery, so the null is well inside noise. Thinking mode is disabled, so the result is stated as a lower bound on format-driven separation, not an upper one.

## What we take from it
- **Method (adopt as a gate):** a *label-source equivalence check* before any classification claim — if our white-box model predicts a category and that category is sourced from one dataset, report the accuracy of predicting the dataset instead; equal numbers mean the model is reading provenance.
- **Method (adopt as a control):** residualisation as a first-class ablation. Fit a linear map from surface features to the activation, subtract it, and re-measure. If the effect dies, the effect was the surface.
- **Measure:** the gradient figure — 100% raw, 93%-ish after count-only control, chance after full format regression — is more informative than any single number, because it says *how much* of an apparently perfect result is surface.
- **Warning (sharpest of all):** a 100% probe with beautiful geometry, separated clusters and low hull contamination can be entirely an artifact. Every geometric descriptor they compute is also produced by pure format. Clean geometry is not evidence; only geometry that survives deconfounding is.
- **Warning:** the causal arm's null is compatible with "no signal" and with "weak signal, n = 15"; a 40.0% vs 31.7% gap at p = 0.286 should be read as "not demonstrated", never as "demonstrated absent", and the same asymmetry will show up in our own small-n intervention studies.
- **Framing:** the paper's uniform-strategy conclusion is itself under-powered (one model, one anchor set) — it is the weakest part of the entry — but the methodological apparatus is separable from it and is the part worth importing.
